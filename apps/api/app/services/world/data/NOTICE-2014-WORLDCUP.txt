openfootball worldcup.json — the 1986–2022 FIFA World Cups

The files beside this notice are unmodified copies of the openfootball community's
`worldcup.json` for the 1986, 1990, 1994, 1998, 2002, 2006, 2010, 2014, 2018 and 2022
FIFA World Cups.

Attribution (required by the licence, and given here): the match data, scores, goals and venues
in these files were compiled by the openfootball project, an open-data football results archive.
The figures this product prints from them (Mexico 1986: 132 goals, 12 venues; Italy 1990: 115 goals,
14 venues; United States 1994: 141 goals, 9 venues; France 1998: 171 goals, 10 venues; Korea & Japan
2002: 161 goals, 20 venues; Germany 2006: 147 goals, 12 venues; South Africa 2010: 145 goals,
10 venues; Brazil 2014: 171 goals, 12 venues and the 1–7 semi-final; Russia 2018: 169 goals,
13 venues; Qatar 2022: 172 goals, 8 venues) are derived from these files.

Scorer coverage in the archive varies by year: the 1990 file transcribes goal minutes for eight
entries in five matches; the 1986, 1994, 1998, 2002, 2006 and 2010 files transcribe none. This
product does not merge or invent scorer rows: it prints scorer tables only where the committed
file carries one and labels every scorer claim from curator knowledge otherwise. The 1998 absence
of a scorer list is the reason the final at Stade de France prints without a Golden Boot table;
every scorer cell on this tour is honest about what the bytes carry.

This product does not modify these files. Its synthetic world is a separate universe and shares
none of these figures. Any discrepancy between these files and any other account of the
tournaments is the reader's call to settle with the source, not with us.
