openfootball worldcup.json — the 2014, 2018 and 2022 FIFA World Cups

The files beside this notice are unmodified copies of the openfootball community's
`worldcup.json` for the 2014, 2018 and 2022 FIFA World Cups.

Source:   https://github.com/openfootball/worldcup.json
Files:    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openfootball/worldcup.json/master/2014/worldcup.json

Attribution (required by the licence, and given here): the match data, scores, goals and
venues in these files were compiled by the openfootball project, an open-data football
results archive. The figures this product prints from them (Brazil 2014: the final at
Maracanã, 171 goals, 12 venues and the 1–7 semi-final; Russia 2018: the final at Luzhniki,
169 goals, 13 venues; Qatar 2022: the final at Lusail Iconic Stadium, 172 goals, 8 venues)
are derived from these files.

This product does not modify these files. Its synthetic world is a separate universe and
shares none of these figures. Any discrepancy between these files and any other account of
the tournaments is the reader's call to settle with the source, not with us.
