openfootball worldcup.json — the 2002, 2006, 2010, 2014, 2018 and 2022 FIFA World Cups

The files beside this notice are unmodified copies of the openfootball community's
`worldcup.json` for the 2002, 2006, 2010, 2014, 2018 and 2022 FIFA World Cups.

Attribution (required by the licence, and given here): the match data, scores, goals and venues
in these files were compiled by the openfootball project, an open-data football results archive.
The figures this product prints from them (Korea & Japan 2002: the final at Yokohama, Germany the
runners-up, 161 goals, 20 venues; Germany 2006: the final at the Olympiastadion, 147 goals,
12 venues; South Africa 2010: the final at Soccer City, 145 goals, 10 venues;
Brazil 2014: the final at Maracanã, 171 goals, 12 venues and the 1–7 semi-final; Russia 2018:
the final at Luzhniki, 169 goals, 13 venues; Qatar 2022: the final at Lusail Iconic Stadium,
172 goals, 8 venues) are derived from these files.

The 2002 and 2010 snapshots transcribe fixtures and scorelines without goal minutes or scorer
lists; the 2006 snapshot carries goal minutes only for nine entries in four matches. This product
does not merge or invent those rows: the doors print scorer tables only where the committed file
carries one and label every scorer claim from curator knowledge otherwise.

This product does not modify these files. Its synthetic world is a separate universe and shares
none of these figures. Any discrepancy between these files and any other account of the
tournaments is the reader's call to settle with the source, not with us.
