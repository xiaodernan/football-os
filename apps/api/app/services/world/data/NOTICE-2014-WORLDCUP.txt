`worldcup.json` (FIFA World Cups) and `euro.json` (UEFA European Championships).

Attribution (required by the licence, and given here): the match data, scores, goals and venues
in these files were compiled by the openfootball project, an open-data football results archive.
The figures this product prints from them are derived from these files, nothing else: ten World
Cups (Mexico 1986 through Qatar 2022, 132–172 goals each as the files spell them) and two
European Championships — Euro 2024 (Spain beat England 2–1 at Berlin; 117 goals) and Euro 2021,
the postponed 2020 tournament (Italy beat England 3–2 on penalties at Wembley after 1–1; 142
goals).

Scorer coverage in the FIFA archive varies by year: the 1990 file transcribes goal minutes only
for eight entries in five matches; the 1986, 1994, 1998, 2002, 2006 and 2010 files transcribe
none. The two euro.json files transcribe every goal. This product does not merge or invent
scorer rows: it prints scorer tables only where the committed file carries one and labels every
scorer claim from curator knowledge otherwise.

This product does not modify these files. Its synthetic world is a separate universe and shares
none of these figures. Any discrepancy between these files and any other account of the
tournaments is the reader's call to settle with the source, not with us.
