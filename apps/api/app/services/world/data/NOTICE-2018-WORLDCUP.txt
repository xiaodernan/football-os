openfootball worldcup.json — 2022 and 2018 FIFA World Cups

The two files beside this notice (`openfootball-worldcup-2018.json`,
`openfootball-worldcup-2022.json`) are unmodified copies of the openfootball
community's `worldcup.json` for the 2018 and 2022 FIFA World Cups.

Source:   https://github.com/openfootball/worldcup.json
Files:    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openfootball/worldcup.json/master/2018/worldcup.json
          https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openfootball/worldcup.json/master/2022/worldcup.json

Attribution (required by the licence, and given here): the match data, scores, goals and
venues in these files were compiled by the openfootball project, an open-data football
results archive. The figures this product prints from them (Russia 2018: the final at
Luzhniki, 169 goals, 13 venues, and the 2022 World Cup: the final at Lusail Iconic Stadium,
172 goals, 8 venues) are derived from these files.

This product does not modify the files. Its synthetic world is a separate universe and
shares none of these figures. Any discrepancy between these files and any other account
of either tournament is the reader's call to settle with the source, not with us.
