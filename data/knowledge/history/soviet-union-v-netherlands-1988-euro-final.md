## The short answer

The 1988 European Championship final was played at the Olympiastadion in Munich on 25 June 1988. The Netherlands beat the Soviet Union 2-0 to claim their first European title. Referee: Michel Vautrot (France). Attendance: 62,770.

## The road to the final

Both finalists came through Group 2. The Soviet Union topped it and beat Italy in the semi-final; the Netherlands finished second and beat hosts West Germany in the semi-final, reaching their first European final.

## How the match unfolded

The Netherlands controlled the final and won 2-0, a victory crowned by a famous long-range goal — Marco van Basten's volley from an angle on the right, one of the greatest goals in tournament history.

## What the final meant

- The Netherlands' first and only European Championship title.
- The peak of the Ajax-inspired Dutch school of the late 1980s.
- Van Basten's volley became an enduring image of the competition.
