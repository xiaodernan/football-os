## The short answer

The 1993–94 UEFA Champions League final was played at the Olympic Stadium in Athens on 18 May 1994 at 20:15 EEST — before 70,000 witnesses. AC Milan 4–0 FC Barcelona. Goals: Daniele Massaro 22', 45+2'; Dejan Savićević 47'; Marcel Desailly 58'. Referee Philip Don (England). Linesmen Rob Harris and Roy Pearson; fourth official Martin Bodenham.

Milan's second consecutive Champions League — their fourth European Cup/Champions League in all. Desailly became the first player to win the trophy in consecutive seasons with two different clubs (Marseille 1993, Milan 1994).

## What the route told us

Milan: First round Aarau 1–0 (1–0 A, 0–0 H); Second round Copenhagen 7–0 (6–0 A, 1–0 H); Group B winners — Anderlecht 0–0 A, Porto 3–0 H, Werder Bremen 2–1 H and 1–1 A, Anderlecht 0–0 H, Porto 0–0 A; Semi-final 3–0 over Monaco (second leg, 3–0 at home).

Barcelona: First round Dynamo Kyiv 5–4 (1–3 A, 4–1 H); Second round Austria Wien 5–1 (3–0 H, 2–1 A); Group A winners — Galatasaray 0–0 A, Monaco 2–0 H, Spartak Moscow 2–2 A and 5–1 H, Galatasaray 3–0 H, Monaco 1–0 A; Semi-final 3–0 over Porto (second leg, 3–0 at home).

The injuries on Milan's side made them underdog on paper — van Basten and Gianluigi Lentini (£13m, the then-world record) were out injured; Baresi and Costacurta suspended; the UEFA three-foreigners rule left Capello without Răducioiu, Papin or Brian Laudrup. On Barcelona's side Cruyff left Michael Laudrup out of the final entirely — Capello called that "his mistake" after the match.

## How the match decided itself

Milan led from 22 minutes. Savićević drove down the right, slipped Massaro through to tap into an open goal, 1–0. Just before half-time Roberto Donadoni ran hard down the left, crossed for Massaro, who banged in his second, 2–0. Savićević slipped Miguel Ángel Nadal and lobbed Zubizarreta in the 47th, 3–0. Two minutes after Savićević hit a post, Desailly beat the offside trap and made it 4–0. Barcelona never produced the reply.

## What the final meant

- **The last great European Cup decade closing itself out** — Milan, Cruyff's Dream Team, Romário, Stoichkov, Guardiola, Koeman — headline columns that even an injury list could not dilute.
- **The all-white final** — Milan's customary final kit, and Cruyff's red-and-blue reproduced.
- **The record margin** — until Paris Saint-Germain 5–0 Inter in 2025, the 1994 Athens game stood as the largest-margin European final in the Champions League era.
- **Desailly's double-consecutive continental crown at different clubs** — Marseille 1993, Milan 1994, a leap no one else has made with the trophy.
