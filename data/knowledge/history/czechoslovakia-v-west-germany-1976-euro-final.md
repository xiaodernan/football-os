## The short answer

The 1976 European Championship final was played at the Stadion Crvena Zvezda in Belgrade, Yugoslavia, on 20 June 1976. Czechoslovakia drew 2-2 with West Germany after extra time and won 5-3 on penalties. Referee: Sergio Gonella (Italy). Attendance: 30,790.

## The road to the final

Czechoslovakia topped their qualifying group, beat the Soviet Union 4-2 on aggregate and the Netherlands 3-1 after extra time in the semi-final. West Germany beat Spain and hosts Yugoslavia 4-2 after extra time.

## How the match unfolded

The final finished 2-2 after extra time, forcing a penalty shoot-out. Czechoslovakia won 5-3, the winning kick taken by Antonin Panenka, who gently chipped the ball down the middle — a style of penalty that now bears his name.

## What the final meant

- Czechoslovakia's only European Championship title.
- The birth of the "Panenka" penalty, one of football's most famous innovations.
- West Germany's only major-tournament shoot-out defeat for decades.
