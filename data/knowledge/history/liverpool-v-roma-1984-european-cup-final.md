## The short answer

The 1983–84 European Cup final was played at the Stadio Olimpico, Rome (Roma's home), on 30 May 1984 at 20:15 CEST, before 69,693 witnesses. Liverpool 1–1 Roma after extra time; Liverpool won 4–2 on penalties. Goals: Phil Neal 13' (Liverpool), Roberto Pruzzo 42' (Roma). Despite the equaliser, Liverpool won the shoot-out 4–2. Referee Erik Fredriksson (Sweden).

## What the route told us

Liverpool: English champions. Odense Boldklub 6–0; Athletic Bilbao 1–0 (Ian Rush's goal, San Mamés); Benfica 5–1; semi-finals Dinamo București 3–1 (Souness broke Lică Movilă's jaw in the first leg, two away wins for Rush).

Roma: Italian champions (Runners-up in Serie A but cup winners). IFK Göteborg 4–2; CSKA Sofia 2–0; Dynamo Berlin 4–2; semi-finals Dundee United 3–2 (a 2–0 away deficit to 3–0 at home, and later reveleations about match-fixing corruption).

## How the match decided itself

Liverpool opened with Phil Neal (13'); Roma drew level with Roberto Pruzzo (42'). 120 minutes with no further goals; the match went to penalties. Liverpool scored four — Neal, Souness, Rush, Kennedy; Roma scored two (Di Bartolomei, Righetti). Nicol missed over the bar; Conti and Graziani missed for Roma. Liverpool 4–2, fourth European Cup.

## What the final meant

This was Liverpool's fourth European Cup in eight years (1977, 1978, 1981, 1984) — unmatched across Europe. Joe Fagan retired at the end of the season on a public stage. The 1985 final, at Heysel, ended in tragedy — and Liverpool could not defend their crown.
