## The short answer

The 2017 CONCACAF Gold Cup final was played at Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara, California, on 26 July 2017. The United States beat Jamaica 2-1. Referee: Walter López (Guatemala). Attendance: 63,032. Jordan Morris was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Jamaica stunned Mexico in the semi-final to reach their first Gold Cup final. The United States, playing at home, were seeking a sixth title in a tournament they had long dominated.

## How the match unfolded

The match was tightly contested before Jordan Morris scored a late winner to give the United States a 2-1 victory. The goal sealed a sixth Gold Cup crown for the hosts and denied Jamaica a historic first title.
