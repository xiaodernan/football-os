## The short answer

The 2013 FIFA Confederations Cup final was played at Estádio do Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, on 30 June 2013. Brazil beat Spain 3-0. Referee: Björn Kuipers (Netherlands). Attendance: 73,531. Neymar was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Brazil reached their fifth Confederations Cup final and third in succession, after previous wins in 1997, 1999, 2005 and 2009. Spain reached their first Confederations Cup final, arriving on a record run of 29 competitive matches without defeat.

## How the match unfolded

Fred scored twice and Neymar once as Brazil won 3-0, ending Spain's unbeaten record. The Brazilian forwards constantly troubled the Spanish defence, and the scoreline reflected a dominant display.

## What the final meant

- Brazil's third consecutive and fourth overall Confederations Cup title.
- The end of Spain's record of 29 competitive games without defeat.
- Neymar, whose goal and overall play lit up the final, was named man of the match.
- A statement win for the hosts a year before they welcomed the World Cup.

Brazil, managed by Luiz Felipe Scolari, produced a scintillating performance against the side that had won two Euros and a World Cup in the previous five years.
