## The short answer

The 2004 Copa America final was played at the Estadio Nacional in Lima, Peru, on 25 July 2004. Argentina and Brazil drew 2-2, and Brazil won 4-2 on penalties. Referee: Carlos Amarilla (Paraguay). Attendance: 43,000. It was the first Copa final staged in Lima.
