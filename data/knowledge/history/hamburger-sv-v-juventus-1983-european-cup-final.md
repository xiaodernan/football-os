## The short answer

The 1982–83 European Cup final was played at the Olympic Stadium, Athens, on 25 May 1983 at 21:15 EEST, before 73,500 witnesses. Hamburger SV 1–0 Juventus. Goal: Felix Magath 9'. Referee Nicolae Rainea (Romania).

It was Hamburg's first European Cup. It was also Ernst Happel's second European Cup as a manager — he had won the Cup with Feyenoord in 1970. It was the sixth consecutive European Cup final decided by a single goal (1978, 1979, 1980, 1981, 1982, 1983) — a streak featuring Liverpool and Nottingham Forest and Aston Villa in sequence before this one broke in 1984 to a true penalty shoot-out.
