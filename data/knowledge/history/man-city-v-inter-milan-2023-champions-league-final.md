## The short answer

The 2022–23 UEFA Champions League final was played at the Atatürk Olympic Stadium, Istanbul, Turkey, on 10 June 2023 at 22:00 local time — clear night, 21 °C, 54% humidity, before 71,412 witnesses. Manchester City 1–0 Inter Milan. Goal: Rodri 68'. Referee Szymon Marciniak (Poland); assistants Paweł Sokolnicki and Tomasz Listkiewicz; fourth official István Kovács; Var Tomasz Kwiatkowski. UEFA Man of the Match: Rodri.

It was Manchester City's first European Cup and their first UEFA Champions League final to be won — the club's first European major trophy since 1970. They won the Premier League and FA Cup earlier in the season, completing a European treble — only the second by an English club after Manchester United in 1999.

## What the route told us

Manchester City: Group G winners on 14 points (Sevilla 4–0 A opener — Haaland brace; Dortmund 2–1 H; Copenhagen 5–0 H; 0–0 A Copenhagen; 0–0 Dortmund; Sevilla 3–1 H). Knockouts: RB Leipzig 8–1 on aggregate (1–1 away, 7–0 home — five Haaland goals, a single-game Champions League record tied, plus İlkay Gündoğan and Kevin De Bruyne); Bayern Munich 4–1 on aggregate (3–0 H, 1–1 away); Real Madrid 5–1 on aggregate (1–1 away at the Bernabéu, 4–0 home — the rematch after the 6–5 semi-final a season earlier).

Inter Milan: Group C runners-up behind Bayern Munich (Bayern 18, Inter 10, Barcelona 7, Viktoria Plzeň 0). Knockouts: Porto 1–0 on aggregate (1–0 home, 0–0 away at the Estádio do Dragão); Benfica 5–3 on aggregate (2–0 away at the Estádio da Luz in the first leg, 3–3 home in the second — Inter won on aggregate); Milan 3–0 on aggregate (both legs played at the San Siro; Inter won the 'away' leg 2–0 and the 'home' leg 1–0).

## How the match decided itself

Kevin De Bruyne went off after 36 minutes with a hamstring injury — an early absence City's depth still had to absorb. The first half belonged to Inter: Brozović sitting deep, Barella and Çalhanoğlu circling Lautaro Martinez, Onana making two saves to keep City at zero. City held 61% possession but could not turn it into a first-half goal.

In the 68th minute, Bernardo Silva pulled the ball back from the right; Rodri struck left-footed low into the bottom corner — 1–0. Inter pushed; Lautaro shot over the bar in the 59th minute; Dimarco hit the crossbar with three minutes to go and Lukaku blocked his own rebound with the outside of his foot. Ederson saved twice — one in the sixth minute of stoppage time, Gosens.

## What the final meant

It was Manchester City's first European Cup, and their first major European trophy since the 1970 Cup Winners' Cup. They completed the English treble — the second all-English treble, after Manchester United in 1999.

Inter's route was one of the most disciplined of the modern era: through second place in the group behind Bayern Munich; past Porto without conceding; past Benfica in a 5–3 tie; and through Milan in the city derby that decided it.

## The wider meaning

- Turkish hosts had held two finals at the Atatürk Olympic Stadium: the 2005 final between Liverpool and Milan and this one in 2023 — both won by a Premier League club.
- It was Manchester City's first European Cup and first major European trophy in 53 years (since 1970).
