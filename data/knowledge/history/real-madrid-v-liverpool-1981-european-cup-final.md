## The short answer

The 1980–81 European Cup final was played at the Parc des Princes, Paris, on 27 May 1981 at 20:15 CEST — before 48,360 witnesses. Real Madrid 0–1 Liverpool. Goal: Alan Kennedy 82' — off a throw-in from Ray Kennedy on the left, his run into the box beat Rafael García Cortés and placed a shot past Agustín. Referee Károly Palotai (Hungary).

This was Liverpool's third European Cup in four seasons (1977, 1978, 1981). Bob Paisley became the first manager to win the European Cup three times. Real Madrid reached their ninth final — their only defeat in a European final appearance since 1964 — and would go on to win each of the nine finals they played thereafter (as of 2024).

Real Madrid: First round: Limerick (Ireland) 7–2 agg (2–1 A, 5–1 H); Second round: Honvéd 3–0 agg (1–0 H, 2–0 A — Santillana's goal, Cunningham's brace); Quarter-finals: Spartak Moscow 2–0 agg (0–0 A, 2–0 H — Isidro's pair); Semi-finals: Inter 2–1 agg (2–0 H, 0–1 A — Santillana + Juanito goals).

## How the match decided itself

An away Dutch engineered... no. Match mechanics: The first half was even — Liverpool had the drive, Real had the counter, both Lyon good at flank width with Cunningham and Juanito. Neither side scored before the hour.

Then, with Liverpool on the ascendency, Ray Kennedy found Alan Kennedy on the left from a throw-in — Alan Kennedy ran down the line into Real's penalty area, beat Rafael García Cortés, and with the 82nd minute deep for Madrid, put a shot past Agustín. Liverpool settled in for the remainder of the match and held on.

## What the final meant

Three European Cups in five years — 1977, 1978, 1981 — turned Liverpool into the European benchmark of their era; 1984 finished the tally under the same manager. It was Madrid's pause at a Madrid-shaped end of an era — they would not lose another final until 1998 (that defeat, ironically, the start of 14 years of dominance at Real Madrid).
