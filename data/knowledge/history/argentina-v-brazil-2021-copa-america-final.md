## The short answer

The 2021 Copa América final was played at Estádio do Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, on 10 July 2021. Argentina beat hosts and defending champions Brazil 1-0. Referee: Esteban Ostojich (Uruguay). Ángel Di María was named man of the match and scored the only goal in the 22nd minute.

## The road to the final

Argentina had not won a major title since 1993 and had lost three finals in the preceding years. They topped Group A, beat Ecuador 3-0 in the quarter-finals, and edged Colombia 3-2 on penalties after a 1-1 semi-final. Brazil, as hosts and defending champions, had won their group and beaten Chile and Peru in the knockout rounds.

## How the match unfolded

Ángel Di María scored the only goal in the 22nd minute, chipping the ball over Ederson after a breakaway. Argentina then protected the lead with a disciplined defensive display. Brazil pressed late but could not find an equaliser. The win gave Lionel Messi his first senior international trophy.

## What the final meant

- Argentina's fifteenth Copa América title, equalling Uruguay's all-time record.
- A first Copa América since 1993, ending a 28-year trophy drought.
- Lionel Messi's first senior international title, sixteen years into his career.
- Qualification for the CONMEBOL–UEFA Cup of Champions, which Argentina later won against Italy.

## Match details

The final at the Maracanã on 10 July 2021 was refereed by Uruguay's Esteban Ostojich. The crowd was restricted under Covid-19 protocols: the published sources give an announced figure of 6,500 and the match record 7,800, roughly 10% of the stadium's capacity. Di María's 22nd-minute chip was the only goal of the game. Argentina's back line, including Emiliano Martínez in goal, held firm through second-half pressure from a Brazil side led by Neymar and Thiago Silva. Di María was named man of the match. The victory ended Argentina's 28-year wait, gave Messi his first senior international trophy, and set up the CONMEBOL–UEFA Cup of Champions against Italy, which Argentina also won before lifting the 2022 World Cup.
