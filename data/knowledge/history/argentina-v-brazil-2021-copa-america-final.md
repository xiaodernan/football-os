## The short answer

The 2021 Copa America final was played at the Maracana Stadium in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, on 10 July 2021. Argentina beat hosts and defending champions Brazil 1-0. Referee: Esteban Ostojich (Uruguay). Attendance: 6,500. Angel Di Maria was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Argentina went unbeaten through the group stage and the knockout rounds, reaching a first final since 2015. Brazil, hosting and defending the title, also progressed through the tournament to set up a fierce final between the two great rivals of South American football.

## How the match unfolded

Argentina produced a disciplined display to win 1-0. Angel Di Maria scored the only goal, a delicate finish, and Argentina's defence held firm against a Brazil side pushing for an equaliser. The win gave captain Lionel Messi his first senior international trophy.

## What the final meant

- Argentina's fifteenth Copa America title, their first since 1993, ending a twenty-eight-year drought.
- Lionel Messi's first senior international trophy, sixteen years into his career.
- Argentina equalled Uruguay's record of Copa America titles, and went on to win the 2022 World Cup.
