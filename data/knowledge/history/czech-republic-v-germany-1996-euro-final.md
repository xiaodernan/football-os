## The short answer

The 1996 European Championship final was played at Wembley Stadium in London on 30 June 1996. Germany beat the Czech Republic 2-1 via a golden goal in extra time. Referee: Pierluigi Pairetto (Italy). Attendance: 73,611. Oliver Bierhoff scored both German goals.

## The road to the final

Both sides came through Group C, where Germany beat the Czech Republic 2-0. Germany beat Croatia and hosts England on penalties; the Czech Republic beat Portugal and France on penalties.

## How the match unfolded

The Czech Republic took the lead, but Bierhoff equalised and then scored the first golden goal in a European Championship final in the 95th minute, ending the match 2-1 and securing Germany's record third European title.

## What the final meant

- Germany's record third European Championship title.
- The first golden goal to decide a European final.
- Karel Poborsky was named man of the match despite the defeat.
