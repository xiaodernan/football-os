## The short answer

The 2013 Africa Cup of Nations final was played at FNB Stadium in Johannesburg, South Africa, on 10 February 2013. Nigeria beat Burkina Faso 1-0. Referee: Djamel Haimoudi (Algeria). Attendance: 85,000. Mikel John Obi was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Nigeria came through the group stage and knockout rounds to reach another final, with a side determined to add to their honours. Burkina Faso also navigated the tournament to reach a first final in their history, hoping to spring a surprise on the favoured Nigerians.

## How the match unfolded

The final was decided by a single moment of brilliance. Sunday Mba struck a magnificent goal, a ferocious finish from distance that proved the only one of the game. Burkina Faso could not find an equaliser, and Nigeria held on to win 1-0 and lift the trophy.

## What the final meant

- Nigeria's third Africa Cup of Nations title, adding to their honours from 1980 and 1994.
- A famous wondergoal from Sunday Mba that settled the final and entered AFCON folklore.
- Qualification to represent CAF at the 2013 FIFA Confederations Cup in Brazil.

The final at FNB Stadium in Johannesburg on 10 February 2013 was refereed by Djamel Haimoudi of Algeria before 85,000 spectators. The venue was already famous for hosting the 2010 FIFA World Cup final and the 1996 AFCON decider. Nigeria's victory came through Sunday Mba's spectacular strike, a goal from outside the box that flew past the goalkeeper and proved decisive. Burkina Faso, appearing in a first final, battled hard but could not find a way back into the game. Mikel John Obi, named man of the match, anchored the Nigerian midfield.
