## The short answer

The 1989–90 European Cup final was played at the Praterstadion, Vienna, on 23 May 1990 at 20:15 CEST — before 58,000 witnesses. Milan 1–0 Benfica. Goal: Frank Rijkaard 67'. Referee Helmut Kohl (Austria).

This was Milan's fourth European Cup — the second in as many years (Sacchi's back-to-back) — and it was the last European Cup before the competition was rebranded the Champions League the following season.

## Route to the final

Milan: defending champions. First round: HJK Helsinki 5–0 agg (4–0 H, 1–0 A); Second round: Real Madrid 2–1 agg (2–0 H, 0–1 A); Quarter-finals: Mechelen 2–0 (0–0 A, 2–0 H a.e.t.); Semi-finals: Marseille 2–2 agg (1–2 A, 1–0 H — advanced on away goals).

Benfica: First round: Derry City 6–1 agg (2–1 A, 4–0 H); Second round: Budapest Honvéd 9–0 agg (2–0 A, 7–0 H); Quarter-finals: Dnipro 4–0; Semi-finals: Bayern Munich 2–2 agg (1–0 H, 1–2 A — advanced on away goals).

## How the match decided itself

Rijkaard broke through the Benfica defence in the 67th minute and scored — a purposeful penetration that decided the match. Milan defended deep the rest of the way and saw it out.

## What the final meant

- Milan became the first back-to-back European champions since Bayern (1974, 1975, 1976 counted as three-in-a-row). Milan were the last club to defend the European Cup — until Real Madrid in 2017.
- It was also the European Cup's twilight before the Champions League era began in 1992.
- The 1988/89 and 1989/90 Milan sides are often cited as among the greatest club teams ever.
- Benfica, although beaten, reached their fifth European Cup final, losing four times (1963, 1965, 1988, 1990).
