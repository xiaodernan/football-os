## The short answer

The 1972–73 European Cup final was played at the Red Star Stadium, Belgrade, Yugoslavia, on 30 May 1973 at 20:30 — before 89,484 witnesses, the competition's first final ever in Yugoslavia. Ajax 1–0 Juventus. Goal: Johnny Rep 5'. Referee Milivoje Gugulović (Yugoslavia); assistants Ratko Čanak and Petar Kostovski.

Ajax secured their third European Cup in as many years — the first club ever to win three consecutive European Cups (1971, 1972, 1973) — in the golden era of Total Football. Juventus were on their first final in any European competition.

## How the match decided itself

Johnny Rep scored after five minutes: Cruyff delivered from the left; Rep finished. Juventus chased the game for the next 85 minutes; Ajax held the lead and ran out winners.

## What the final meant

Ajax completed their three-peat European dynasty. By the rules then in force, they kept the European Cup trophy permanently — Ajax was the first club to win three times and keep the cup outright (Real Madrid aside). In 1995-96 UEFA changed the rule to ensure the trophy was returned. Juventus finally struck back at Ajax 23 years later in the 1996 Champions League final in Rome, winning after the final went 1–1 a.e.t., 4–2 on penalties.
