## The short answer

The UEFA Euro 2024 final was played at the Olympiastadion in Berlin, Germany, on 14 July 2024. Spain beat England 2-1. Referee: François Letexier (France). Attendance: 65,600. Nico Williams was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Spain reached their fifth European Championship final with a perfect tournament — winning all seven matches, including a 2-1 quarter-final win over hosts Germany and a 2-1 semi-final against France. England reached a second successive Euro final, beating Slovakia, Switzerland and the Netherlands along the way.

## How the match unfolded

The first half finished goalless. In the 47th minute, Lamine Yamal crossed low for Nico Williams to score into the right corner. Cole Palmer equalised in the 73rd minute with a low shot from outside the box. In the 86th minute, substitute Mikel Oyarzabal finished from Marc Cucurella's cross; a tight offside check confirmed the goal stood, giving Spain a 2-1 win.

## What the final meant

- Spain's record-breaking fourth European Championship title, surpassing Germany as the competition's sole most successful nation.
- Their third title in the last five editions.
- Spain won all seven matches and scored fifteen goals, a new record for a single European Championship.
- England became the first side to lose consecutive European Championship finals.
