## The short answer

The 2021 CONCACAF Gold Cup final was played at Allegiant Stadium in Paradise, Nevada, on 1 August 2021. The United States beat Mexico 1-0 after extra time. Referee: Saíd Martínez (Honduras). Attendance: 61,114. Matt Turner was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

The United States, as hosts, reached their ninth Gold Cup final. Mexico were defending champions and appeared in a record seventh final against the USA, having won five of the previous six meetings in the final (1993, 1998, 2009, 2011, 2019); the USA's only win had been in 2007.

## How the match unfolded

The match stayed goalless through ninety minutes. In the 117th minute of extra time, Miles Robinson headed in from a corner to give the United States a 1-0 lead — one of the latest winners in the tournament's history. The score held to the final whistle.

## What the final meant

- The United States' seventh Gold Cup title.
- A first final win over Mexico since 2007, ending a run of five straight final losses to their rivals.
- Matt Turner, whose saves kept Mexico at bay, was named man of the match.
- The win came in extreme heat — 99 °F (37 °C) at kick-off.

It was the seventh Gold Cup final between the USA and Mexico, and the second in succession.
