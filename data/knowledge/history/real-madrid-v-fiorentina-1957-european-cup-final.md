## The short answer

The 1956–57 European Cup final was played at the Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid, on 30 May 1957 at 17:30 CET — before 124,000 witnesses. Real Madrid 2–0 Fiorentina. Goals: Alfredo Di Stéfano 69' (penalty), Paco Gento 75'. Referee Leo Horn (Netherlands).

Real Madrid's second consecutive European Cup — the first time a Spanish club had successfully defended the title.

## What the route told us

Real Madrid: First round: Rapid Wien 5–5 agg (4–2 H, 1–3 A; replay at the Bernabéu 2–0); Quarter-finals: Nice 6–2 agg (3–0 H, 3–2 A); Semi-finals: Manchester United 5–3 agg (3–1 H, 2–2 A at Old Trafford).

Fiorentina: First round: IFK Norrköping 2–1 agg (1–1 H, 1–0 H); Quarter-finals: Grasshopper 5–3 agg; Semi-finals: Red Star Belgrade 1–0 agg (1–0 first leg, 0–0 second leg).

## How the match decided itself

In a fiery second-half, Di Stéfano converted a 69th-minute penalty awarded for a foul on Enrique Mateos near the area line — the lineman had signalled offside, but Leo Horn ignored and gave the penalty. Gento scored six minutes later, at 75 minutes of play. 2–0. Fiorentina never recovered.

## What the final meant

Real Madrid's second consecutive European Cup — their dynasty of dominance officially begun. Madrid founded their five-in-a-row run; they won again in 1958 and 1959 to make three yet.
