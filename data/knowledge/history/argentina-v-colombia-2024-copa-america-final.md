## The short answer

The 2024 Copa America final was played at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, Florida, on 14 July 2024. Defending champions Argentina beat Colombia 1-0 after extra time. Referee: Raphael Claus (Brazil). Attendance: 65,300. Angel Di Maria was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Argentina reached their eighth final, extending their own record, and came in as defending champions with fifteen titles. Colombia reached their third final, having last won the trophy in 2001, and hoped to add a second crown.

## How the match unfolded

The final was decided in extra time when Argentina found the only goal, a late strike that sealed a 1-0 win. Colombia, who had been competitive throughout, could not find an equaliser, and Argentina held on to claim a record-breaking sixteenth title.

## What the final meant

- Argentina's record sixteenth Copa America title, their third consecutive major tournament triumph after the 2021 Copa and 2022 World Cup.
- A feat matched only by Spain's run of 2008, 2010 and 2012, and Argentina's own 1945-1947 run.
- The match was marred by a kickoff delay of over an hour due to crowd disorder outside the stadium.

## Match details

The final at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens on 14 July 2024 was refereed by Raphael Claus of Brazil before 65,300 spectators. The night was marred by chaotic scenes outside the stadium, which delayed kickoff by more than an hour as thousands of ticketless fans tried to force their way in. When play finally began, Argentina's experience told as they found the only goal in extra time to win 1-0. Angel Di Maria, the man of the match, capped another memorable performance. The victory gave Argentina a record sixteenth Copa America title and completed a third consecutive major tournament triumph, following the 2021 Copa and the 2022 World Cup, a run achieved only by Spain and Argentina themselves in the 1940s.
