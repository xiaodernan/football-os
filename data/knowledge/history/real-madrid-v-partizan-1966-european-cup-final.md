## The short answer

The 1965–66 European Cup final was played at the Heysel Stadium, Brussels, on 11 May 1966 at 19:30 CET, before 46,745 witnesses. Real Madrid 2–1 FK Partizan. Goals: Amancio 70', Fernando Serena 76' for Madrid; Velibor Vasović 55' for Partizan. Referee Rudolf Kreitlein (West Germany).

This was Real Madrid's sixth European Cup — in 11 years of the competition's existence, the only time Madrid drank from the trophy with anyone else since 1962. Paco Gento remains the only Madrid player to win all six European Cups as a player.

## What the route told us

Real Madrid: 1965–66 La Liga champions. Preliminary round: Feyenoord 6–2 agg (1–2 A, 5–0 H); First round: Kilmarnock 7–3 agg (2–2 A, 5–1 H); Quarter-finals: Anderlecht 4–3 agg (0–1 A, 4–2 H); Semi-finals: Manchester United 2–1 agg (2–0 H, 0–1 A — Manchester won the second leg at the Bernabéu? Actually no, Manchester won the first leg 0–1 at Old Trafford and lost 2–0 at the Bernabéu; Madrid went through 2–1 on aggregate).

Partizan: Yugoslav champions (the Yugoslav league was one entity). Preliminary round: Nantes 4–2 agg (2–0 H, 2–2 A); First round: Werder Bremen 3–1 agg (3–0 H, 0–1 A); Quarter-finals: Sparta Prague 6–4 agg (1–4 A, 5–0 H — Partizan came from four down in the tie); Semi-finals: Internazionale 2–1 agg (1–0 H, 1–1 A).

## How the match decided itself

Partizan gave themselves the lead in the 55th minute through Vasović (who was also a libero, not a striker). Madrid equalised five minutes later through Amancio on 70 minutes. Nine minutes later, Serena scored the winner in the 76th. Real Madrid lifted their sixth European Cup.

## What the final meant

This was Real Madrid's sixth European Cup — their first in four years (1962). Madrid's next title came in 1981.

Paco Gento played in all the Madrid European Cup finals through this period — the defining winger of Madrid's dynasty.

Partizan remain the only Yugoslav/Serbian club to reach the European Cup final until 1991 Red Star Belgrade.
