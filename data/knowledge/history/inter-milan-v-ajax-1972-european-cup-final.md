## The short answer

The 1971–72 European Cup final was played at De Kuip, Rotterdam, on 31 May 1972, before 61,354 witnesses. Inter Milan 0–2 Ajax. Goals: Johan Cruyff 47', 78'. Referee Robert Héliès (France).

Ajax completed their second of three consecutive European Cups — this one at home in Holland under Ștefan Kovács. Cruyff, the architect of Total Football, was named Man of the Match. Inter — finalists four times in nine years (1964 W, 1965 W, 1967 L, 1972 L) — lost their fourth and their last thirty-year window of European prominence until 2010.

## How the match decided itself

The match had no goals in the first half; Inter's catenaccio held until after the interval. Cruyff scored in the 47th minute — the pivot of the tie. Inter, dragged wide by the chasing play, could not answer on the counter. Cruyff scored a second in the 78th — a composed finish after a classic Total Football build-up. The 1972 final is cited as Total Football's greatest single match.

## What the final meant

The dynasty: 1971 Wembley — 1972 De Kuip — 1973 Belgrade. Cruyff, Neeskens, Keizer, Haan, Mühren — the Total Football spine through two coaches (Michels then Kovács) in one timeline.
