## The short answer

The Copa America Centenario final was played at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, on 26 June 2016. Argentina and Chile drew 0-0 after extra time, and Chile won 4-2 on penalties. Referee: Heber Lopes (Brazil). Attendance: 82,026. Claudio Bravo was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

The Centenario was a special edition marking a hundred years of Copa America. Argentina and Chile met in Group D, with Argentina winning 2-1, but both progressed to a rematch in the final, the second consecutive Copa decider between the two.

## How the match unfolded

The final was a tense, goalless contest through extra time, decided by penalties. Chile won 4-2, converting their kicks while Argentina missed, retaining the Copa in a repeat of the previous year's shoot-out ending.

## What the final meant

- Chile's second consecutive Copa America title, cementing their golden generation's legacy.
- A rematch of the 2015 final, with the same shoot-out outcome.
- After the defeat and having missed the first penalty, Lionel Messi announced his retirement from international football, though he later reversed the decision.

## Match details

The final at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford on 26 June 2016 was refereed by Heber Lopes of Brazil before 82,026 spectators. It was the 45th Copa America final and a rematch of the 2015 decider, with the two sides having also met in Group D, where Argentina won 2-1. Once again the final went to penalties after a 0-0 draw, and once again Chile prevailed, this time 4-2, as Argentina missed kicks. Claudio Bravo, the man of the match, was central to Chile's success. The defeat prompted Lionel Messi to announce his retirement from international football after missing Argentina's first penalty and suffering a third consecutive final defeat, a decision he later reversed.
