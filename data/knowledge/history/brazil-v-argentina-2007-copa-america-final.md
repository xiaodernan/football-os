## The short answer

The 2007 Copa America final was played at the Estadio Jose Pachencho Romero in Maracaibo, Venezuela, on 15 July 2007. Brazil beat Argentina 3-0. Referee: Carlos Amarilla (Paraguay). Attendance: 40,000. Goals came from Julio Baptista, an Roberto Ayala own goal, and Dani Alves.

## The road to the final

Brazil navigated a demanding group and knockout path to reach another final, showing the attacking depth that would define the tournament. Argentina, with a star-studded squad, also progressed through the group stage and the knockout rounds to set up a classic final between the two South American giants.

## How the match unfolded

Brazil produced a ruthless display to win 3-0. Julio Baptista opened the scoring, an Roberto Ayala own goal extended the lead, and Dani Alves sealed the result. Argentina, who could have won their fifteenth title, were comprehensively outplayed on the night.

## What the final meant

- Brazil's eighth Copa America title, adding another chapter to their dominance of the competition.
