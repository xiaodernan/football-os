## The short answer

The 2008 European Championship final was played at the Ernst-Happel-Stadion in Vienna, Austria, on 29 June 2008. Spain beat Germany 1-0. Referee: Roberto Rosetti (Italy). Attendance: 51,428. Fernando Torres, the goalscorer, was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Germany finished second in Group B, then beat Portugal in the quarter-final and Turkey in the semi-final. Spain won all three Group D games, beat Italy on penalties in the quarter-final, and beat Russia again in the semi-final.

## How the match unfolded

Spain controlled possession and broke the deadlock in the 33rd minute when Fernando Torres latched onto a through ball, held off the goalkeeper and finished into an empty net. Germany created little in response, and Spain's 1-0 lead held to the final whistle.

## What the final meant

- Spain's second European Championship, and their first since 1964, ending a 44-year wait.
- The beginning of a golden era that would bring the 2010 World Cup and Euro 2012.
- Fernando Torres's goal became one of the most celebrated in the competition's history.
