## The short answer

The 1978–79 European Cup final was played at the Olympiastadion, Munich, on 30 May 1979 at 20:15 CEST — before 68,500 witnesses. Malmö FF 0–1 Nottingham Forest. Goal: Trevor Francis 45+1' — from a John Robertson cross, Francis headed over Jan Möller and into the roof of the net. Referee Erich Linemayr (Austria).

It was Nottingham Forest's first ever European Cup — a club who had won the Football League Cup three times (1978, 1979, 1980) but never the championship. Brian Clough's forest rose from the Second Division only two years before this final. Malmö became the first (and so far only) Nordic club to reach a European Cup final.

## What the route told us

Nottingham Forest: English champions. First round: Liverpool (1978 holders) 2–0 agg (2–0 H at the City Ground, 0–0 A); Second round: AEK Athens 7–2 agg (5–1 H, 2–1 A); Quarter-finals: Grasshopper Club Zürich 5–2 agg (4–1 H, 1–1 A); Semi-finals: 1. FC Köln 4–3 agg (3–3 H, 1–0 A).

Malmö FF: Swedish champions. First round: Monaco 1–0 agg; Second round: Dynamo Kiev 2–0 (2–0 H, 0–0 A); Quarter-finals: Wisła Kraków 5–3 agg (4–1 home, 1–2 away); Semi-finals: Austria Wien 1–0 (1–0 home, 0–0 away). Injuries: Bo Larsson and Roy Andersson out; Staffan Tapper (the captain) broke his toe in training the night before the final. They replicated the Club Brugge 1978 blueprint.

## How the match decided itself

Malmö played from inside their own half — a back five shaped tight, mirroring Brugge's 1978 blueprint. Forest fired around but could not break the wall.

In the first-half stoppage time, John Robertson beat two Swedish defenders on the left side and whipped in a cross; Jan Möller did not come for it; Trevor Francis was left unmarked at the far post and headed the ball into the roof of the net at 45+1'.

## What the final meant

Forest — promoted to the top flight in 1977, league champions in 1978, European champions in 1979 — had become an eight-story wonder story under Brian Clough.

Forest would retain the title two seasons later — at the Bernabéu, again 1–0 against Hamburg SV, again Robertson scoring (68'). Clough remains the only manager in European Cup history to win it back-to-back with one club starting from the second tier.
