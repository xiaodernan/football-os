## The short answer

The 1985–86 European Cup final was played at the Ramón Sánchez Pizjuán, Seville (Spain), on 7 May 1986 at 20:15 CEST — before 70,000 witnesses. Barcelona 0–0 Steaua București after extra time; Steaua won 2–0 on penalties. Referee Michel Vautrot (France).

It was the first European Cup final decided on penalties after extra time (no goals from either side across 120 minutes). Helmut Duckadam saved all four Barcelona penalties — every one.

## What the route told us

Barcelona: First round: Sparta Prague 2–2 (2–1 away, 0–1 home on away goals); Second round: Porto 3–3 (2–0 away, 1–3 home; advanced on away goals); Quarter-finals: Juventus 2–1 (1–0 away, 1–1 home); Semi-finals: IFK Göteborg 3–3 (0–3 away, 3–0 home — Barcelona won the tie 5–4 on penalties).

Steaua București: First round: Vejle 5–2 (1–1 away, 4–1 home); Second round: Budapest Honvéd 4–2 (0–1 away, 4–1 home); Quarter-finals: Kuusysi 1–0 (0–0 away, 1–0 home); Semi-finals: Anderlecht 3–1 (0–1 away, 3–0 home).

## How the match decided itself

Neither side scored in 120 minutes. Extra time was goalless. Steaua won 2–0 on penalties — Majearu and Bölöni scored Steaua's two kicks; Barcelona missed all four (Alexanko, Pedraza, Pichi Alonso, Marcos). The final two Steaua penalties (Lăcătuș, Balint) were never needed once the match was decided.

## What the final meant

- Steaua became, and remains, Romania's only European Cup winner.
- They were the first European Cup winner from Eastern Europe — until Red Star Belgrade (1991).
- Helmut Duckadam, the Romanian goalkeeper, saved all four Barcelona penalties. His name has been linked with the legend since — "the wall of Seville".
