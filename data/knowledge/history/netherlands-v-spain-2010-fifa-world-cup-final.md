## The short answer

The 2010 FIFA World Cup final was played at Soccer City (FNB Stadium) in Johannesburg, South Africa, on 11 July 2010. Spain beat the Netherlands 1-0 after extra time. Goal: Andres Iniesta 116' (Spain). Referee: Howard Webb (England). Attendance: 84,490. Iniesta was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

The Netherlands won all three Group E matches and beat Slovakia, Brazil and Uruguay in the knockout rounds. Spain topped Group H and beat Portugal, Paraguay and Germany.

## How the match unfolded

A scrappy, ill-tempered final produced few clear chances. Sergio Ramos and David Villa went close for Spain; Arjen Robben had the best Dutch opportunity, a one-on-one saved by Iker Casillas. John Heitinga was sent off for a second bookable offence in extra time. With four minutes left, Cesc Fabregas supplied Iniesta, whose powerful right-footed shot into the left corner secured a 1-0 win and Spain's first World Cup.

## What the final meant

- Spain's first World Cup title, completing a golden era that also included Euro 2008 and Euro 2012.
- The first World Cup win by a European team outside Europe.
- Iniesta's goal became one of the most celebrated in Spanish football history.
- Casillas won the Golden Glove; the Netherlands lost their third World Cup final without ever winning one.
