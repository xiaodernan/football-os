## The short answer

The 2015 CONCACAF Gold Cup final was played at Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia on 26 July 2015. Mexico beat Jamaica 3-1. Referee: Joel Aguilar (El Salvador). Attendance: 68,930.

## The road to the final

Jamaica reached the first CONCACAF championship final in their history, upsetting the United States in the semi-final. It was Mexico's twelfth Gold Cup final; they were seeking a record tenth title.

## How the match unfolded

Andrés Guardado opened the scoring in the 31st minute, Jesús Corona made it 2-0 just after half-time in the 46th, and Oribe Peralta added a third in the 61st. Darren Mattocks pulled one back for Jamaica in the 79th, but Mexico's 3-1 lead was never in doubt.

## What the final meant

- Mexico's record-breaking tenth Gold Cup title.
- Jamaica's first CONCACAF championship final, reached by beating the USA.
- The first Gold Cup final since 2003 not to feature the United States.
- qualification for a one-match play-off against the USA for a place at the 2017 Confederations Cup.

## Match details

The final at Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia on 26 July 2015 was refereed by El Salvador's Joel Aguilar before 68,930 spectators. Jamaica, coached by Winfried Schäfer, had shocked the USA in the semi-final to reach their first final. Mexico, under Miguel Herrera, controlled the match from the 31st minute when captain Guardado scored. Corona and Peralta extended the lead before Mattocks's 79th-minute reply. The 3-1 victory gave Mexico a record tenth Gold Cup and a play-off against the USA for a 2017 Confederations Cup place.
