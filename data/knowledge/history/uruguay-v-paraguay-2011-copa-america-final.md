## The short answer

The 2011 Copa America final was played at the Estadio Monumental in Buenos Aires, Argentina, on 24 July 2011. Uruguay beat Paraguay 3-0 to earn a record fifteenth Copa America title. Referee: Salvio Fagundes (Brazil). Luis Suarez was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Uruguay came through the tournament in Argentina to reach the final, with Luis Suarez and Diego Forlan among the leading figures. Paraguay also navigated the competition to reach a final, hoping to claim a first Copa title.

## How the match unfolded

Uruguay controlled the final and won 3-0. Suarez, the man of the match, was among the scorers as Uruguay's attacking quality overwhelmed Paraguay. The clean-sheet victory sealed a historic fifteenth title for the visitors.

## What the final meant

- Uruguay's record fifteenth Copa America title, more than any other nation.
- qualification to represent CONMEBOL at the 2013 FIFA Confederations Cup in Brazil.
- Paraguay finished runners-up and earned the Copa Bolivia.
