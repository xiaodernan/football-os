## The short answer

The 1984 European Championship final was played at the Parc des Princes in Paris, France, on 27 June 1984. Hosts France beat Spain 2-0 to claim their first international title. Referee: Vojtech Christov (Czechoslovakia). Attendance: 47,368.

## The road to the final

France qualified automatically as hosts, beat Denmark, Belgium and Yugoslavia in the group, then Portugal in the semi-final. Spain topped their qualifying group and beat Denmark on penalties in the semi-final.

## How the match unfolded

France controlled the final in Paris and won 2-0, with Michel Platini at the heart of a side widely regarded as one of the most attractive teams ever assembled. It was France's first major trophy.

## What the final meant

- France's first international football title.
- Michel Platini's crowning tournament — he scored nine goals in the competition.
- A high-water mark for attacking football in the 1980s.
