## The short answer

The 2001–02 UEFA Champions League final was played at Hampden Park in Glasgow on 15 May 2002 at 20:45 CEST (19:45 BST) — before 50,499 witnesses. Bayer Leverkusen 1–2 Real Madrid. Goals: Raúl 8', Zinedine Zidane 45' (Real Madrid); Lúcio 14' (Bayer Leverkusen). Referee Urs Meier (Switzerland). Zinedine Zidane was named man of the match.
