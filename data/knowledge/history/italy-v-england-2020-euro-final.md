## The short answer

The UEFA Euro 2020 final was played at Wembley Stadium in London on 11 July 2021, having been postponed a year by the COVID-19 pandemic. Italy drew 1-1 with England after extra time and won 3-2 on penalties. Referee: Bjorn Kuipers (Netherlands). Attendance: 67,173. Leonardo Bonucci was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Italy topped Group A and beat Austria, Belgium and Spain en route to their fourth Euro final. England won Group D and beat Germany, Ukraine and Denmark, reaching their first European Championship final.

## How the match unfolded

England's Luke Shaw scored after just two minutes, the fastest goal ever in a Euro final. Bonucci equalised midway through the second half, and the game finished 1-1 after extra time. In the shoot-out England led 2-1 before three consecutive misses, and Italy won 3-2.

## What the final meant

- Italy's first major title since the 2006 World Cup and second European Championship, level with France.
- England's first Euro final, lost on home soil in their second ever major final.
- The fastest goal in a Euro final (Shaw, two minutes) and a dramatic shoot-out finish.

## Match details

The final at Wembley Stadium in London on 11 July 2021 was refereed by Bjorn Kuipers of the Netherlands before 67,173 spectators, a crowd limited by pandemic restrictions. The tournament had been postponed a year from 2020. Shaw's second-minute strike was the fastest goal ever scored in a European Championship final, but Bonucci's second-half equaliser sent the game to extra time and then penalties.
