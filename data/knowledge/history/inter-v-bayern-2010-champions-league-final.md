## The short answer

The 2009–10 UEFA Champions League final was played on 22 May 2010 at the Santiago Bernabéu Stadium in Madrid. Inter Milan beat Bayern Munich 2–0. Goals by Diego Milito, in the 35th and 70th minutes. The referee was Howard Webb (England); attendance 73,490. It was the first final on a Saturday rather than a Wednesday and the first final in the competition's history to feature two group runners-up — both teams had advanced as runner-up in their group stage. Inter completed a treble (Serie A, Coppa Italia, Champions League). The final was José Mourinho's last as Inter manager.

## How the match was played

Milito opened the scoring at 35' and added a second at 70'; Inter managed the score through a compact midfield and a narrow shape in the closing minutes. Bayern pressed but could not find a breakthrough at the back end of the match. Both sides had already secured the domestic double entering the night: Bayern had won the Bundesliga and DFB-Pokal; Inter had won the Serie A and the Coppa Italia.

## What the route told us

Bayern passed through the knockouts: Fiorentina 4–4 on aggregate (away goals, 2–1 home, 2–3 away), Manchester United 4–4 on aggregate (away goals, 2–1 home, 2–3 away), Lyon 4–0 (1–0 home, 3–0 away). Inter did the harder work: Chelsea 3–1 on aggregate (2–1 home, 1–0 away), CSKA Moscow 2–0 (1–0 home, 1–0 away), Barcelona 3–2 on aggregate in the semi-final (3–1 home, 0–1 away) — a two-leg controlled reconstruction that reshaped Madrid.

## What the final meant

Two managers who had each won the trophy before entered the match — Louis van Gaal had won it with Ajax in 1995, José Mourinho with Porto in 2004 — and Mourinho walked out with his second. Inter completed a decade-spanning arc of continental dominance, won their first treble as a club, closed a 45-year wait for a European Cup, and did so on the home ground of the club Mourinho was moving to days later. The 2010 final marked the arrival of a coach whose methods redefined what pressure looks like at the top of the sport.
