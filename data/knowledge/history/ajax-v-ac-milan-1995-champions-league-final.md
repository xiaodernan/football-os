## The short answer

The 1994–95 UEFA Champions League final was played at the Ernst-Happel-Stadion in Vienna on 24 May 1995 at 20:30 CEST — before 49,730 witnesses. Ajax 1–0 AC Milan. Goal: Patrick Kluivert, 85th minute. Referee Ion Crăciunescu (Romania). Linesmen Nicolae Grigorescu and Tudor Constantinescu; fourth official Adrian Porumboiu.

It was the first Champions League final in which players' names were printed on the back of the shirts — and the starball logo appeared on the sleeves for the first time.

## What the route told us

The unusual thing: Ajax and Milan were in the **same Champions League group** (Group D). Ajax won both league-leg meetings — 2–0 at home, 2–0 away — and were docked nothing; Milan were docked two points for crowd trouble in Salzburg on matchday 2, and went to Vienna as group runners-up.

Ajax knockout route: Quarter-finals Hajduk Split 3–0 (0–0 A, 3–0 H); Semi-finals Bayern Munich 5–2 (0–0 A, 5–2 H).
Milan knockout route: Quarter-finals Benfica 2–0 (2–0 H, 0–0 A); Semi-finals Paris Saint-Germain 3–0 agg (1–0 A, 2–0 H).

Ajax's previous finals: 1969 (lost to Milan), 1971, 1972, 1973 (wings of the Cruyff golden age). Milan's: 1958 (lost to Real Madrid), 1963, 1969, 1989, 1990, 1993 (lost to Marseille), 1994.

## How the match decided itself

A tense, flawless defensive game for 84 minutes — Ajax against the European champions in a final that demanded discipline more than fire. Then in the 85th minute, Patrick Kluivert — 18 years and 7 months old — ghosted in at the back stick and slotted home Louis van Gaal's tactical patience. 1–0. Milan never recovered. Milan aimed to win their sixth European Cup and tie Real Madrid's record; instead, Danny Blind's Ajax completed a season in which they beat Milan all three times (twice in the group stage, then here).

## What the final meant

- **Ajax's first title in 22 years** — since the Cruyff-led 1973 triumph in Belgrade.
- **The youngest scorer in Champions League final history** — Kluivert at 18 years and 7 months.
- **Connecting eras** — the starball sleeve badge appeared for the first time; the shirts carried players' names; the Champions League's modern identity completed itself in this match.
- **Milan's involution begins** — Capello left for Real Madrid the following summer, and Milan's next continental revival came under Carlo Ancelotti (2003, 2007).
- **Desailly's still unfinished business** — winner in 1994, loser in this final the following year.
- **Rijkaard the traveller** — Frank Rijkaard, Milan's 1988–1993 engine, stood at the base of Ajax's midfield against his old club.
