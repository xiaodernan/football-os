## The short answer

The UEFA Euro 2024 final was played at the Olympiastadion in Berlin, Germany, on 14 July 2024. Spain beat England 2-1 to win a record-breaking fourth European Championship. Referee: Francois Letexier (France). Attendance: 65,600. Nico Williams was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Spain won all seven matches, beating Croatia, Italy, Albania, Georgia, Germany, France and in the final England, setting a new tournament record of fifteen goals. England reached their second consecutive Euro final, beating Slovakia, Switzerland, the Netherlands and Denmark along the way.

## How the match unfolded

Spain took the lead through Nico Williams, England equalised through Cole Palmer, and the game stayed level until Mikel Oyarzabal scored a late winner to make it 2-1. Spain held on for a record fourth title, their third in five editions.

## What the final meant

- Spain's record fourth European Championship, overtaking Germany as the sole record holder.
- The first side to win all seven matches in a 24-team Euro, with a record fifteen goals.
- England became the first team to lose two consecutive European Championship finals.
