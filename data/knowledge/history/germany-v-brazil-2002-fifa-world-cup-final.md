## The short answer

The 2002 FIFA World Cup final was played at the International Stadium in Yokohama, Japan, on 30 June 2002. Brazil beat Germany 2-0. Goals: Ronaldo 67' and 79' (Brazil). Referee: Pierluigi Collina (Italy). Attendance: 69,029. Ronaldo was named man of the match; Germany's Oliver Kahn won the Golden Ball.

## The road to the final

Germany topped Group E and beat Paraguay, the United States and hosts South Korea in the knockout rounds. Brazil won all three Group C matches, then beat Belgium, England and Turkey.

## How the match unfolded

A goalless first half gave way to Brazil's second-half passage. Ronaldo opened the scoring on 67 minutes, capitalising on a spill by Kahn that the goalkeeper later called his "only mistake in the finals". Twelve minutes later Ronaldo struck again: Kleberson moved forward, Rivaldo let the ball run through his legs, and Ronaldo controlled and fired low into the bottom corner. Brazil held on for 2-0.

## What the final meant

- Brazil's record fifth World Cup title, a tally no other nation has matched.
- Ronaldo's redemption after 1998, finishing the tournament as top scorer and man of the match of the final.
- The first World Cup final contested by two of the traditional giants in Asia, and the last for Germany as a runner-up until 2008.
- Brazil remained the most recent champion to win every match of a tournament.
