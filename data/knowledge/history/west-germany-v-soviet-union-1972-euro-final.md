## The short answer

The 1972 European Championship final was played at the Heysel Stadium in Brussels, Belgium, on 18 June 1972. West Germany beat the Soviet Union 3-0. Referee: Ferdinand Marschall (Austria). Attendance: 43,437.

## The road to the final

West Germany topped their qualifying group, beat England over two legs and hosts Belgium in the semi-final. The Soviet Union won their group, beat Yugoslavia and Hungary.

## How the match unfolded

West Germany controlled the final from start to finish, scoring three goals without reply to claim their first European Championship in emphatic fashion.

## What the final meant

- West Germany's first European Championship title.
- The Soviet Union's third final, their second defeat.
- The start of West Germany's era as a European powerhouse.
