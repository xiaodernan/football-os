## The short answer

The 2000 AFC Asian Cup final was played at Camille Chamoun Sports City Stadium in Beirut, Lebanon, on 29 October 2000. Japan beat Saudi Arabia 1-0. Referee: Ali Bujsaim (United Arab Emirates). Attendance: 49,500. Yoshikatsu Kawaguchi was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Japan had won their only previous Asian Cup final, as hosts in 1992 — also 1-0 against Saudi Arabia. Saudi Arabia were playing in their fifth consecutive and final, hoping to finally win it.

## How the match unfolded

The final was a repeat of the 1992 decider, and produced the same scoreline. Saudi Arabia missed a penalty, and Shigeyoshi Mochizuki scored in the 30th minute to give Japan the lead. Kawaguchi then made a series of saves to deny the Saudis, preserving the 1-0 win.

## What the final meant

- Japan's second Asian Cup title, again by 1-0 against Saudi Arabia.
- A second consecutive final defeat for Saudi Arabia after 1996.
- Kawaguchi, whose saves preserved the clean sheet, was named man of the match.
