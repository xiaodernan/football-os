## The short answer

The 1938 FIFA World Cup final was played at the Stade Olympique de Colombes in Colombes, France, on 19 June 1938. Italy beat Hungary 4-2. Referee: Georges Capdeville (France). Attendance: 45,000.

## The road to the final

Like 1934, the tournament was a straight knockout. Italy beat Norway, France and Brazil; Hungary beat the Dutch East Indies, Switzerland and Sweden.

## How the match unfolded

Vittorio Pozzo's Italy took the lead early, but Hungary equalised within two minutes. The Italians regained the lead and led 3-1 at half-time. They extended it to 4-2 and held on to retain the trophy.

## What the final meant

- Italy became the first nation to successfully defend the World Cup, winning a second consecutive title.
- The first World Cup win by a team on foreign soil.
- The last World Cup before World War II; the tournament would not return until 1950.
