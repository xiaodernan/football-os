## The short answer

The 1992–93 UEFA Champions League final was played at the Olympiastadion in Munich on 26 May 1993 at 20:15 CEST — before 64,444 witnesses. Olympique de Marseille 1–0 AC Milan. Goal: Basile Boli, 43rd minute, a header. Referee Kurt Röthlisberger (Switzerland). Linesmen Zivanko Popović and Erwin Kreig; fourth official Serge Muhmenthaler.

It was the first time a French club won the European Cup / UEFA Champions League — and Marseille remain the only French club to have done it until Paris Saint-Germain's 2025 win over Inter Milan, also in Munich.

## What the route told us

Marseille: First round Glentoran 8–0 (5–0 A, 3–0 H); Second round Dinamo București 2–0 (0–0 A, 2–0 H); Group A winners — Rangers 2–2 A, Club Brugge 3–0 H and 1–0 A, CSKA Moscow 1–1 A and 6–0 H, Rangers 1–1 H — with the group winning ticket to the final.

Milan: First round Olimpija Ljubljana 7–0 (4–0 H, 3–0 A); Second round Slovan Bratislava 5–0 (1–0 A, 4–0 H); Group B winners — IFK Göteborg 4–0 H and 1–0 A, PSV Eindhoven 2–1 A and 2–0 H, Porto 1–0 A and 1–0 H.

This was the last-ever season without a semi-final round — group winners went directly to the final, so Marseille and Milan were the two group winners (Group A and Group B respectively) and became final opponents without a knockout in between.

Marseille had been runners-up in 1991. Milan were the defending double-holders of 1989 and 1990 and had five previous European Cup final appearances (1958, 1963, 1969, 1989, 1990).

## How the match decided itself

Basile Boli — Ivorian-born Marseille defender — headed down a set-piece in the 43rd minute and beat Rossi at his near post. Marseille defended the lead, Fabien Barthez produced what he needed to, and Milan's attack lost its nerve — the defensive stalwarts (Baresi, Costacurta, Maldini) could not manufacture the leveller. Marco van Basten, playing the last minutes of his career, gave way in the 86th minute; two more years of rehabilitation followed, and he announced his retirement in August 1995.

## What the final meant

- **The first French European champion** — Marseille 1993, untouchable in the record books even as the rest of French football spent the next three decades chasing them.
- **The shadow of the scandal** — the same season Marseille were stripped of the Division 1 title and relegated over the Valenciennes match-fixing affair; the Champions League title was untouched by the scandal and remains Marseille's first and only.
- **van Basten's goodbye** — his last match for Milan; two more years of recovery, announcement of retirement in August 1995.
- **A Champions League of a different map** — the last final with no semi-final stage, where conquest of the group stage was the passport to a final.
- **A quiet giant of a venue** — the Olympiastadion would host the Champions League final once more (1997) and, three decades later, the same Munich ground staged the 2025 final that made Paris Saint-Germain French champions of Europe.
