## The short answer

The 2004 European Championship final was played at the Estadio da Luz in Lisbon, Portugal, on 4 July 2004. Underdogs Greece beat hosts Portugal 1-0. Referee: Markus Merk (Germany). Attendance: 62,865. Theodoros Zagorakis was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Both finalists came through Group A and even met in the tournament's opening game, which Greece won 2-1. Portugal beat Russia and Spain, then England on penalties and the Netherlands in the semi-final. Greece beat defending champions France and then the Czech Republic via a silver goal in the semi-final.

## How the match unfolded

Greece scored the only goal in the 57th minute, Angelos Basinas's corner met by Angelos Charisteas, who headed powerfully past the goalkeeper. Greece held on for a 1-0 win, completing a shock victory in only their second tournament appearance, having never before won a match at a major event.

## What the final meant

- Greece's first major international title, widely called the greatest upset in European Championship history.
- Only the second time the same two nations played both the opening game and the final of one tournament.
- Otto Rehhagel's disciplined, organised side confounded the odds of 150-1 before the tournament.

## Match details

The final at the Estadio da Luz in Lisbon on 4 July 2004 was refereed by Markus Merk of Germany before 62,865 spectators. Greece and Portugal had already met in the opening game of the tournament, Greece winning 2-1 in what the BBC labelled a shock defeat for the hosts. Greece's knockout run included beating defending champions France and then the Czech Republic via a silver goal.
