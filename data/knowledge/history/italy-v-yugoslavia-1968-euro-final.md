## The short answer

The 1968 European Championship final was decided over two matches at the Stadio Olimpico in Rome. The first, on 8 June, finished 1-1 after extra time (68,817 spectators); the replay on 10 June saw Italy win 2-0 (32,886 spectators), their first European title.

## The road to the final

Italy topped their qualifying group and reached the final via a coin toss after a goalless semi-final with the Soviet Union. Yugoslavia beat France 6-2 on aggregate and England 1-0 in the semi-final.

## How the match unfolded

The first final ended 1-1 even after extra time, forcing a replay two days later. In the replay Italy were decisive, winning 2-0 to lift the trophy on home soil.

## What the final meant

- Italy's first European Championship title.
- The only European final ever decided by a replay.
- Yugoslavia's second final defeat in three editions.
