## The short answer

The 2022 FIFA World Cup final was played at Lusail Stadium in Lusail, Qatar, on 18 December 2022, with kick-off in the evening local time — before 88,966 witnesses. Argentina 3–3 France after extra time; Argentina won 4–2 on penalties. Goals: Lionel Messi 23' pen, 108', Ángel Di María 36' (Argentina); Kylian Mbappé 80' pen, 81', 118' pen (France). Referee Szymon Marciniak (Poland). Lionel Messi was named man of the match.

It was Argentina's third FIFA World Cup title and their first since 1986. France, the defending champions, fell short in a final that became one of the most dramatic in the competition's history.

## What the route told us

Argentina (Group C winners): lost 2–1 to Saudi Arabia, then beat Mexico 2–0 and Poland 2–0. Knockout stage: Australia 2–1 (round of 16), Netherlands 2–2 after extra time, 4–3 on penalties (quarter-final), Croatia 3–0 (semi-final).

France (Group D winners): beat Australia 4–1 and Denmark 2–1, lost 1–0 to Tunisia. Knockout stage: Poland 3–1 (round of 16), England 2–1 (quarter-final), Morocco 2–0 (semi-final).

## How the match decided itself

Messi converted a penalty in the 23rd minute to give Argentina the lead, and Di María finished a sweeping move in the 36th minute for 2–0. France struggled to threaten for much of the match until Mbappé scored from the penalty spot in the 80th minute and equalised 97 seconds later with a volley. Extra time brought another swing: Messi put Argentina ahead again in the 108th minute, but Mbappé completed his hat-trick with a penalty in the 118th minute to make it 3–3.

In the shoot-out, Argentina prevailed 4–2. Emiliano Martínez saved from Kingsley Coman, Aurélien Tchouaméni missed the target, and Gonzalo Montiel scored the decisive penalty to seal the title.

## What the final meant

- **Argentina's third world title** — joining 1978 and 1986, and ending a 36-year wait.
- **Messi's crowning moment** — he scored twice in the final, won man of the match, and captained Argentina to the trophy.
- **Mbappé's hat-trick in defeat** — he became only the second man to score three goals in a World Cup final, yet finished on the losing side.
- **A final of extremes** — from Argentina's control to France's late surge, extra-time swings, and penalties, it is widely remembered as an all-time classic.
