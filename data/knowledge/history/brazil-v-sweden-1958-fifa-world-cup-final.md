## The short answer

The 1958 FIFA World Cup final was played at Rasunda Stadium in Solna, near Stockholm, Sweden, on 29 June 1958. Brazil beat hosts Sweden 5-2 to win their first World Cup. Referee: Maurice Guigue (France). Attendance: 49,737.

## The road to the final

Brazil topped Group 4 (3-0 Austria, 0-0 England, 2-0 Soviet Union) and beat Wales and France (5-2) in the knockout rounds. Sweden topped Group 3 and beat the Soviet Union and West Germany.

## How the match unfolded

Brazil swept Sweden aside in a 5-2 win that remains the highest-scoring World Cup final. The 17-year-old Pele scored twice, becoming the youngest goalscorer in a final, and Vava added two more. Sweden's Nils Liedholm became the oldest goalscorer in a final at 35. It was the first final between a European and an American side.

## What the final meant

- Brazil's first World Cup title, the start of a dynasty.
- The arrival of Pele on the world stage, at 17 years and 249 days.
- Sweden's best-ever World Cup finish; they remain the only host nation to lose a final.
- The only World Cup staged in Europe and won by a non-European team.
