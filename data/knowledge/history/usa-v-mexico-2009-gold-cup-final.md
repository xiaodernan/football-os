## The short answer

The 2009 CONCACAF Gold Cup final was played at Giants Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, on 26 July 2009. Mexico beat the United States 5-0. Referee: Courtney Campbell (Jamaica). Attendance: 79,156. Giovani dos Santos was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

The USA and Mexico, the region's two powers, both reached another final in their long rivalry. Mexico were aiming for a fifth Gold Cup, the USA for a sixth, in front of a huge crowd in the New York area.

## How the match unfolded

The USA were reduced to ten men when Jay Heaps was sent off in the 24th minute. Mexico did not score until the 56th, when Gerardo Torrado converted a penalty, but then added four more: Giovani dos Santos in the 62nd, Carlos Vela in the 67th, José Antonio Castro in the 79th and Guillermo Franco in the 90th, for a 5-0 rout.

## What the final meant

- Mexico's fifth Gold Cup and eighth continental title overall.
- One of the most one-sided finals in the tournament's history.
- Giovani dos Santos, whose goal and creativity stood out, was named man of the match.

## Match details

The final at Giants Stadium on 26 July 2009 was refereed by Jamaica's Courtney Campbell before 79,156 spectators. The USA, managed by Bob Bradley, were reduced to ten men in the 24th minute when Jay Heaps was dismissed. Despite the advantage, Mexico, coached by Javier Aguirre, took until the 56th minute to break the deadlock through Torrado's penalty. Goals from dos Santos, Vela, Castro and Franco followed in a ruthless 5-0 rout. Dos Santos was named man of the match. It was Mexico's fifth Gold Cup and eighth continental crown overall.
