## The short answer

The 2018 FIFA Club World Cup final was played at Zayed Sports City Stadium in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, on 22 December 2018. Real Madrid beat Al Ain 4-1. Referee: Jair Marrufo (United States). Attendance: 40,696. Marcos Llorente was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Real Madrid, defending champions and UEFA Champions League winners, were seeking a third consecutive title. Al Ain represented the host nation as UAE Pro-League champions and became the first Emirati club to reach a Club World Cup final, notably beating River Plate in the semi-final.

## How the match unfolded

Luka Modrić opened the scoring in the 14th minute. Marcos Llorente made it 2-0 in the 60th, Sergio Ramos added a third in the 79th, and Al Ain's Yahia Nader put through his own goal in stoppage time for 4-0. Tsukasa Shiotani pulled one back for Al Ain in the 86th minute, making the final score 4-1.

## What the final meant

- Real Madrid's third consecutive and fourth overall Club World Cup title, overtaking Barcelona as outright record winners.
- A fourth global crown in five years for the Spanish club.
- Al Ain's historic run as the first UAE club to reach the final.
- Llorente, who scored and was tireless in midfield, was named man of the match.

The final at Zayed Sports City Stadium in Abu Dhabi on 22 December 2018 was refereed by the USA's Jair Marrufo before 40,696 spectators. Real Madrid, managed by Santiago Solari, controlled the match from the outset, with Modrić's 14th-minute strike setting the tone.
