## The short answer

The 1979–80 European Cup final was played at the Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid, on 28 May 1980 at 20:30 CEST — before 51,000 witnesses. Nottingham Forest 1–0 Hamburger SV. Goal: John Robertson 21' — dribbled past Kaltz? No — fifteen feet from the box, Robertson picked out the curl first-time low shot. Referee António Garrido (Portugal).

It established Nottingham Forest as back-to-back champions — 79 Munich, 80 Madrid — Clough's small village team carrying the biggest trophy in club football, two years into First Division life.

## How the match decided itself

Hamburger was supposed to be the favourite — Keegan (now in his first season in Germany) alongside Felix Magath. But Forest needed only 15 minutes of patience. John Robertson collected a loose ball outside the box and squeezed a low strike past Rudi Kargus into the near corner; 1–0 Forest. Hamburg tried second half, but Forest held on.

## What the final meant

Two consecutive. Clough had 1978 league title, 1979 League Cup, 1979 European Cup, 1980 League Cup, 1980 European Cup — and then 1980 Intercontinental Cup against Nacional (Uruguay). Forest's complete short run in football history began there.
