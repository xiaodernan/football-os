## The short answer

The 2025–26 UEFA Champions League final was played on 30 May 2026 at the Puskás Aréna in Budapest, with a 18:00 CEST kickoff — three hours earlier than any previous final — between defending champions Paris Saint-Germain and Arsenal. It ended 1–1 after extra time, and PSG won the penalty shoot-out 4–3 to complete back-to-back titles, becoming the second side in the competition's history to do it (Real Madrid went back-to-back in 2016–17).

## How the match was played

In the 6th minute, Marquinhos's attempted clearance deflected off Trossard onto Havertz's left, and the striker beat Safonov from close range to make it 1–0 Arsenal. Arsenal never threatened again — one shot on target all match. In the 61st minute, Mosquera fouled Kvaratskhelia in the penalty box; Dembélé converted the penalty to square it. In the 77th minute, Kvaratskhelia's effort flew off the post. By full-time PSG had 18 attempts (four on target) vs Arsenal's four (one on target). Extra time produced an appeal for a penalty against Nuno Mendes on Madueke — the referee refused it and saw Rice and Arteta booked for the protest.

## The shoot-out

Gonçalo Ramos opened for PSG, Gyökeres answered for Arsenal; Désiré Doué scored for PSG. Arsenal's Eze missed wide left. Raya then saved Nuno Mendes's attempt for Arsenal. Four converted in a row — Rice, Hakimi, Martinelli, Beraldo — leaving PSG 4–3 in front. Gabriel Magalhães sent his attempt over the bar, and PSG completed the Budapest coronation. Vitinha was named man of the match.

## What the route told us

PSG reached the final as the 11th-placed league-phase finisher — and walked through it anyway: Monaco 5–4 in the play-off, Chelsea 8–2 in the round of 16, Liverpool 4–0 in the quarter-final, Bayern 6–5 in the semi-final. Arsenal finished first in the league phase, then 3–1 Bayer Leverkusen, 1–0 Sporting, 2–1 Atlético Madrid. Both finalists were league champions — the first time since 2020. This was the first final between two capital-city clubs since 1971, and the first with a titleholder present since 2018. PSG were the first French club to reach three finals, and the first to appear in consecutive finals since Liverpool 2018–19.

## What the final meant

PSG now hold two consecutive Champions Leagues (2025 5–0 vs Inter, 2026 penalties vs Arsenal), matching Real Madrid's feat in 2016–17. Arsenal lost their second European final (2006 vs Barcelona), remaining the only undefeated finalist never to win it. Vitinha anchored midfield as MOTM, and the 18:00 kickoff — three hours earlier than before — was widely noted as a fan-focused change by UEFA. For the reader, this final completes the modern trilogy: Munich 2025 (5–0, the longest route to a debut title), Budapest 2026 (1–1, pens, the first back-to-back for a non-Real Madrid side), and Real Madrid's eleven-year shadow over the previous decade.
