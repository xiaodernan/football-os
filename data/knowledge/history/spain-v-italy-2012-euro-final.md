## The short answer

The 2012 European Championship final was played at the Olympic Stadium in Kyiv, Ukraine, on 1 July 2012. Defending champions Spain beat Italy 4-0. Referee: Pedro Proenca (Portugal). Attendance: 63,170. Andres Iniesta was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Spain topped Group C with a draw against Italy and wins over Croatia and the Republic of Ireland, then beat France and Portugal (on penalties). Italy finished second in the same group and beat England on penalties and Germany in the semi-final.

## How the match unfolded

Spain produced a ruthless performance, scoring four goals to win 4-0 — the greatest margin of victory in a European Championship final. Italy, who had drawn with Spain in the group, were comprehensively outplayed in the decider.

## What the final meant

- Spain's third European title, a joint record, and the first team to win two consecutive European Championships.
- The first team to win three consecutive major tournaments (Euro 2008, World Cup 2010, Euro 2012).
- A 4-0 scoreline that remains the largest winning margin in a Euro final.

## Match details

The final at the Olympic Stadium in Kyiv on 1 July 2012 was refereed by Pedro Proenca of Portugal before 63,170 spectators. Spain and Italy had already met in Group C, drawing there, but the final was a one-sided affair as Spain's possession game overwhelmed Italy. The 4-0 result was the biggest winning margin in the history of European Championship finals. It gave Spain a joint-record third European title and made them the first side to win consecutive European Championships, capping a run of three successive major tournaments won without conceding a single goal in any knockout stage.
