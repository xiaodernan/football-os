## The short answer

The 2017–18 UEFA Champions League final was played at the NSC Olimpiyskiy Stadium in Kyiv, Ukraine, on 26 May 2018 at 21:45 local time (UTC+3) under sunny 20 °C weather, before 61,561 spectators. Real Madrid beat Liverpool 3–1 to complete a third consecutive Champions League title — the first team in the Champions League era to defend twice — and to claim an unmatched thirteenth European Cup. Goalscorers: Karim Benzema 51' (the ball deflected off Loris Karius's handling and into the net), Gareth Bale 63' (a bicycle kick from a Marcelo cross) and 83' (a 40-yard shot that beat Karius), for the three-goal haul. Sadio Mané levelled for Liverpool in the 55th minute (James Milner's corner, Dejan Lovren's header, Mané's tap-in). Mohamed Salah left the pitch in the first half with a dislocated shoulder after a lock-arm challenge by Sergio Ramos at around the 25th minute. Referee Milorad Mažić (Serbia); Player of the match: Gareth Bale (two goals as a substitute); attendance 61,561.

## How the match was played

Liverpool started both the brighter opening: Trent Alexander-Arnold's low shot through a defender's legs at 23' produced a late Keylor Navas save, and Liverpool's possession-based counterattack unsettled Madrid's slower buildup in the first half. In the 25th minute Ramos locked Salah's arm, forcing an awkward fall; Salah was diagnosed with a dislocated shoulder and left the pitch in the 29th minute, replaced by Adam Lallana. Dani Carvajal left with a hamstring injury in the 37th minute. Madrid were unlucky at the end of the first half — Benzema got on the end of a Cristiano Ronaldo header but the goal was ruled offside and halftime arrived goalless.

The second half belonged to the substitutes as much as anyone. Madrid opened the scoring at 51' — Benzema deflected a throw-in by Liverpool goalkeeper Loris Karius back into the net. Liverpool hit back through Mané at 55' (Milner's corner, Lovren's header, Mané's tap-in), and Madrid then brought Gareth Bale on for Isco at 61'; two minutes later Bale completed a bicycle kick from a Marcelo cross, and the stadium emptied itself onto its feet. Liverpool mounted pressure — Mané hit the post; there were appeals for a handball penalty that were not given; Madrid absorbed it — and Bale scored again in the 83rd minute from 35–40 yards out, the ball swerving in front of Karius to slip under his reaching hands. Bale, a substitute off the bench, braced at Kyiv in his third consecutive European Cup final.

## What the route told us

Liverpool's route: play-off round as runners-up in the 2016–17 Premier League table (fourth) — 6–3 on aggregate against Hoffenheim in the qualifying rounds (2–1 away, 4–2 home); Group E winners on 12 points despite 2–2 at Sevilla and 3–3 away to the same side, then Liverpool's 5–0 outing at Porto in the R16, 5–1 vs Manchester City in the QF (3–0 home, 2–1 away), and 7–6 vs Roma in the SF (5–2 at Anfield first leg). Real Madrid finished Group H runners-up behind Tottenham (16 pts for Spurs vs 13 for Madrid), eliminated PSG 5–2 on aggregate (3–1 at home, 2–1 away), eliminated Juventus 4–3 on aggregate (3–0 at Turin, 1–3 at the Bernabéu), and eliminated Bayern Munich 4–3 on aggregate in the semi-final (2–1 away, 2–2 at home — the second leg ended with a Real Madrid equaliser on 90 minutes).

## What the final meant

This was Madrid's fourth European Cup in five seasons — 13th overall, an all-time record; for Liverpool it was an eighth final appearance (wins 1977, 1978, 1981, 1984, 2005; losses 1985 and 2007), and their first since 2007; also the first Champions League final to feature an English side since 2012. For Kyiv, it was the city's first Champions League final — its venue had hosted Euro 2012 (Spain beat Italy 4–0) and matches at the 1980 Summer Olympics. For Madrid's dynasty it was the third consecutive European Cup in different dress: Ramos and Carvajal left with their third consecutive title, Bale's MOTM night with two goals sealed his name in the club archive, and Kyiv mounted the club's record 16th European Cup final appearance.
