## The short answer

The 2006 Africa Cup of Nations final was played at Cairo International Stadium in Cairo, Egypt, on 10 February 2006. Hosts Egypt drew 0-0 with Ivory Coast after extra time and won 4-2 on penalties. Referee: Mourad Daami (Tunisia). Attendance: 74,100.

## The road to the final

Egypt, playing on home soil, navigated the tournament to reach another final with a side determined to add to their honours. Ivory Coast also progressed through the competition to contest the decider, arriving with a talented squad ranked above their hosts in the world standings.

## How the match unfolded

The final was a tense, goalless contest through ninety minutes and extra time. Neither side could break the deadlock, so the title was decided by a penalty shoot-out. Egypt held their nerve to win 4-2, securing a record fifth Africa Cup of Nations title in front of their own supporters.

## What the final meant

- Egypt's fifth AFCON title, a record at the time and their fourth in six years.
- A dominant run on home soil that confirmed Egypt as the leading force in African football.
- A heartbreaking defeat for Ivory Coast, whose talented generation again fell short in a major final.
