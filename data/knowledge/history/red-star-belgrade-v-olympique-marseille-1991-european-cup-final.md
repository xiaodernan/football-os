## The short answer

The 1990–91 European Cup final was played at the Stadio San Nicola, Bari, on 29 May 1991 at 20:15 CEST — before 51,587 witnesses. Red Star Belgrade (Crvena zvezda) 0–0 Marseille after extra time; they won 5–3 on penalties. Referee Tullio Lanese (Italy).

## What the route told us

Red Star Belgrade: First round: Grasshoppers 5–2 agg (1–1 A, 4–1 H); Second round: Rangers 4–1 agg (3–0 H, 1–1 A); Quarter-finals: Dynamo Dresden 6–0 (3–0 away, 3–0 home); Semi-finals: Bayern Munich 4–3 agg (2–1 away, 2–2 home).

Marseille: First round: Dinamo Tirana 5–1 (5–1 H, 0–0 A); Second round: Lech Poznań 8–4 agg (2–3 A, 6–1 H); Quarter-finals: AC Milan 4–1 agg (1–1 A, 3–0 H — the giant-killer knockout); Semi-finals: Spartak Moscow 5–2 agg (3–1 A, 2–1 H).

This is the only European Cup/Champions League final where both clubs were debutants — that remains true as of 2026.

## How the match decided itself

120 minutes, zero goals. The shootout: Red Star's five takers (Prosinečki, Binić, Belodedici, Mihajlović, Pančev) all scored — 5 in 5 taken. Marseille's four takers: Amoros missed the second kick of the shootout; Casoni, Papin, and Mozer all scored — 3 in 4 taken. Red Star won the shootout 5–3.

## What the final meant

- Red Star's first (and only) European Cup.
- The Yugoslav Wars started in June 1991, two weeks after this final. The South Slavic side that won the European Cup was made up from a country that no longer existed weeks later.
- Red Star won as the only both-debutants European Cup final in history.
