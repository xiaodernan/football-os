## The short answer

The 1995–96 UEFA Champions League final was played at the Stadio Olimpico in Rome on 22 May 1996 at 20:30 CEST — before 70,000 witnesses. Ajax 1–1 Juventus after extra time; Juventus won 4–2 on penalties. Goals: Fabrizio Ravanelli 12' (Juventus), Jari Litmanen 41' (Ajax). Referee Manuel Díaz Vega (Spain). Linesmen Joaquín Olmos González and Manuel Fernando Tresaco Gracia; fourth official José María García-Aranda.

It was Juventus's second European Cup/Champions League title (after 1985) and the end of Ajax's title defence. As of 2026, this remains the last time a Dutch club reached a Champions League final.

## What the route told us

Ajax (defending champions): Group D winners — Real Madrid 1–0 H and 2–0 A, Ferencváros 5–1 A and 4–0 H, Grasshopper 3–0 H and 0–0 A. Quarter-finals Borussia Dortmund 3–0 agg (2–0 A, 1–0 H). Semi-finals Panathinaikos 3–1 agg (0–1 H, 3–0 A).

Juventus: Group C winners — Borussia Dortmund 3–1 A and 1–2 H, Steaua București 3–0 H and 0–0 A, Rangers 4–1 H and 4–0 A. Quarter-finals Real Madrid 2–1 agg (0–1 A, 2–0 H). Semi-finals Nantes 4–3 agg (2–0 H, 2–3 A).

The final was a rematch of the 1973 European Cup final, which Ajax had won 1–0.

## How the match decided itself

Ravanelli put Juventus ahead in the 12th minute, capitalising after Frank de Boer's header fell loose and Edwin van der Sar was caught advancing. Ajax answered before half-time: Litmanen tapped in the rebound from a free kick in the 41st minute after Angelo Peruzzi could only parry. The second half and extra time produced no further goals despite chances at both ends.

In the shoot-out, Juventus scored all four: Ciro Ferrara, Gianluca Pessotto, Michele Padovano and Vladimir Jugović. For Ajax, Edgar Davids missed first, Litmanen and Arnold Scholten scored, and Sonny Silooy missed last — Jugović's kick decided it 4–2.

## What the final meant

- **Juventus back on the European summit** — a second title, nine years after their previous Champions League campaign and 11 years after their first European Cup (1985).
- **Ajax's defence ends in Rome** — the 1995 champions reached a second straight final but could not retain the trophy.
- **A Dutch endpoint, for now** — as of 2026, no Dutch club has returned to a Champions League final since Ajax in 1996.
- **A historic pairing repeated** — Ajax and Juventus met in the European Cup final for the second time, 23 years after 1973.
