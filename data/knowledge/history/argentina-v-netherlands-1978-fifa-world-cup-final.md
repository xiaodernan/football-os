## The short answer

The 1978 FIFA World Cup final was played at the Estadio Monumental in Buenos Aires, Argentina, on 25 June 1978. Hosts Argentina beat the Netherlands 3-1 after extra time. Referee: Sergio Gonella (Italy). Attendance: 71,483. Mario Kempes, the tournament's top scorer, was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Argentina finished second in their opening group, then won Group B and beat Poland in the semi-final. The Netherlands finished second in their opening group, topped Group A, and beat Italy in the semi-final.

## How the match unfolded

The Netherlands, appearing in their second straight final, faced the hosts in a hostile Monumental. Argentina took the lead through Mario Kempes, the Netherlands equalised late to force extra time, but Kempes scored again and Daniel Bertoni sealed a 3-1 win. It was Argentina's first World Cup, won on home soil.

## What the final meant

- Argentina's first World Cup title, after losing the 1930 final.
- The Netherlands' second consecutive final defeat, both times to the host nation (1974 West Germany, 1978 Argentina).
- Mario Kempes finished as tournament top scorer and final man of the match.
