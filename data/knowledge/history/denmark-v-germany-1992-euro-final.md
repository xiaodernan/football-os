## The short answer

The 1992 European Championship final was played at Nya Ullevi in Gothenburg, Sweden, on 26 June 1992. Denmark beat Germany 2-0. Referee: Bruno Galler (Switzerland). Attendance: 37,800. Goals: John Jensen and Kim Vilfort.

## The road to the final

Denmark qualified only ten days before the tournament, called up when Yugoslavia was ejected following the breakup of the country. They finished second in their group, beat the Netherlands on penalties in the semi-final, and stunned holders Germany in the final.

## How the match unfolded

Denmark scored through John Jensen and Kim Vilfort to win 2-0 and complete one of the greatest upsets in international football, beating a German side that had won the World Cup two years earlier.

## What the final meant

- Denmark's first and only European Championship title.
- The ultimate "fairytale" — a team that almost did not enter winning the trophy.
- Considered one of the biggest shocks in the history of the sport.
