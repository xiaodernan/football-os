## The short answer

The 1991–92 European Cup final was played at Wembley Stadium, London, on 20 May 1992 at 19:15 BST — before 70,827 witnesses. Barcelona 1–0 Sampdoria after extra time. Goal: Ronald Koeman 112' — a classic left-foot free kick over the wall, past the goalkeeper into the net. Referee Aron Schmidhuber (Germany).

It was the last European Cup final before the competition was rebranded the Champions League in 1992-93.

Barcelona: First round: Hansa Rostock 3–1 (3–0 home, 0–1 away); Second round: 1. FC Kaiserslautern 3–3 agg (2–0 home, 1–3 away — on away goals); Group: Group B winner — Sparta Prague, Benfica, Dynamo Kyiv; Finished first with 9 points.

Sampdoria: First round: Rosenborg 7–1 (5–0 home, 2–1 away); Second round: Kispest Honvéd 4–3 (1–2 away, 3–1 home); Group: Group A winner — Red Star Belgrade, Panathinaikos, Anderlecht.

## How the match decided itself

The first 90 minutes were goalless with end-to-end football. In extra time, the goal came from the left: a Koeman free kick, around 20 yards out, struck between the massed wall and beat the goalkeeper. Sampdoria never recovered.
