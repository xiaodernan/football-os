## The short answer

The 2024 Copa América final was played at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, Florida, on 14 July 2024. Argentina beat Colombia 1-0 after extra time. Referee: Raphael Claus (Brazil). Attendance: 65,300. Ángel Di María was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Argentina, the defending champions, reached an eighth Copa América final, extending their own record. Colombia were in their third final, first since winning in 2001. Argentina had won 15 titles before the match; the victory would make it a record 16.

## How the match unfolded

Colombia threatened early, with Luis Díaz hitting the post in the 5th minute and Jhon Córdoba doing likewise a minute later. Lionel Messi was substituted in the 66th minute after an ankle injury. The match stayed 0-0 through ninety minutes and into extra time. In the 112th minute, Lautaro Martínez — one of three substitutes introduced at the start of extra time — ran clear and finished past Camilo Vargas for the only goal.

## What the final meant

- Argentina's record-breaking 16th Copa América title.
- A third consecutive major tournament triumph, following the 2021 Copa América and 2022 World Cup — a feat previously achieved only by Spain (2008–2012) and Argentina's own 1945–1947 side.
- Di María, the veteran winger, was named man of the match.
- Qualification for the 2025 Finalissima against Spain, the Euro 2024 winners.
