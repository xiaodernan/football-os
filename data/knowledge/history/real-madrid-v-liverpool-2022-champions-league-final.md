## The short answer

The 2021–22 UEFA Champions League final was played at the Stade de France, Saint-Denis, on 28 May 2022 at 21:36 — delayed 36 minutes by pre-match security chaos — partly cloudy night, 18 °C, 45% humidity, before 75,000 witnesses. Liverpool 0–1 Real Madrid. Goal: Vinícius Júnior 59' (Valverde pass). Referee Clément Turpin (France); assistants Nicolas Danos and Cyril Gringore; VAR Jérôme Brisard. UEFA Man of the Match: Thibaut Courtois — nine saves in a Champions League final, an Opta record since 2003–04.

Real Madrid's 14th European Cup — a record-extending championship, their fifth in nine seasons, their eighth Champions League final win in a row, and Ancelotti's fourth as a manager — the first to win four (2003 Milan, 2007 Milan, 2014 Real Madrid, 2022 Real Madrid).

## What the route told us

Liverpool: Group B winners on 18 points — Milan 3–2 H, Porto 5–1 A, Atlético Madrid 3–2 A, Atlético Madrid 2–0 H, Porto 2–0 H, Milan 2–1 A. Knockouts: Inter Milan 2–1 on aggregate (2–0 away first leg, 0–1 at home); Benfica 6–4 on aggregate (3–1 away first leg, 3–3 at home); Villarreal 5–2 on aggregate (2–0 home, 3–2 away at El Madrigal).

Real Madrid: Group D winners on 15 points (Inter 1–0 away, Sheriff 1–2 home, Shakhtar 5–0 away, 2–1 home, Sheriff 3–0 away, Inter 2–0 home). Knockouts: Paris Saint-Germain 3–2 on aggregate (0–1 away, 3–1 home); Chelsea 5–4 on aggregate (Madrid won 3–1 away at Stamford Bridge but lost 2–3 at home in extra time); Manchester City 6–5 on aggregate (3–3 at the Etihad — Madrid trailed late and Rodrygo scored twice — then 3–1 at the Bernabéu in extra time).

## How the match decided itself

Kick-off delayed 36 minutes by crowd-control failures outside the stadium (France's Interior Minister later admitted he had got the security arrangements wrong and apologised to Liverpool fans). Benzema had a first-half goal disallowed for offside — a three-minute and twenty-second VAR review of the validity of the goal. Courtois made nine saves — the most in a Champions League final since Opta began keeping records in 2003–04.

Goal: in the 59th minute, Federico Valverde ran down the right and crossed low; Vinícius Júnior tucked past Alisson with the outside of his right foot. Liverpool out-shot Real Madrid 23–3 (shots on target 9–1). Despite the margin, Madrid defended it with one.

## What the final meant

It was Real Madrid's 14th European Cup — twice as many as the next-best team (Milan, seven). Carlo Ancelotti became the first manager to win four Champions League titles, and his eighth European trophy overall. It was Klopp's third final loss (Dortmund 2013, Liverpool 2018, 2022 — Lippi holds the joint record with three). Nine players secured their fifth Champions League title — Marcelo, Modrić, Kroos, Benzema, Casemiro, Carvajal, Isco, Bale and Nacho.

Liverpool were appearing in their tenth European Cup final. Real Madrid's semifinal had been a 6–5 aggregate epic against Manchester City — Madrid were 3–1 down late in the first leg at the Etihad and came back to draw 3–3 through Rodrygo's stoppage-time double, then won 3–1 in Madrid after extra time.
