## The short answer

The 1987–88 European Cup final was played at the Neckarstadion, Stuttgart, on 25 May 1988 at 20:15 CEST — before 64,000 witnesses. PSV Eindhoven 0–0 Benfica after 120 minutes; PSV won 6–5 on penalties. Referee Luigi Agnolin (Italy).

It was PSV Eindhoven's first (and only) European Cup — the first by a Dutch team since Feyenoord in 1970; Benfica's first European Cup since 1962.

## What the route told us

PSV: First round: Galatasaray 3–2 (3–0 home, 0–2 away); Second round: Rapid Wien 4–1 (2–1 away, 2–0 home); Quarter-finals: Girondins de Bordeaux 1–1 agg (1–1 away, 0–0 home — advanced on away goals); Semi-finals: Real Madrid 1–1 (1–1 away, 0–0 home — advanced on away goals).

Benfica: First round: Partizani Tirana 4–0 (4–0 home); Second round: AGF 1–0 (0–0 away, 1–0 home); Quarter-finals: Anderlecht 2–1 (2–0 home, 0–1 away); Semi-finals: Steaua București 2–0 (0–0 away, 2–0 home).

## How the match decided itself

Neither team could find a winner — defensive mastery, cautious football — and extra time remained 0–0. The match went to penalties.

PSV's six takers (Ronald Koeman, Wim Kieft, Ivan Nielsen, Gerald Vanenburg, Søren Lerby, Anton Janssen) scored all six. Benfica's five out of six scored (Elzo, Dito, Redouane, Pacheco, Mozer); the sixth, António Veloso, had his effort saved by PSV's Hans van Breukelen — the decisive moment in the final. PSV won 6–5 to lift their only European Cup.

## What the final meant

- PSV won the Dutch treble that season — KNVB Cup, Eredivisie, European Cup — the first Dutch club to do it.
- They were the first Dutch side to win the European Cup since Feyenoord in 1970.
- The five PSV players who featured that night also played for Netherlands in UEFA Euro 1988, won in West Germany in June 1988 — the only major international trophy Oranje has ever lifted.
- PSV's European Cup stands alone as their only one.
