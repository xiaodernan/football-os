## The short answer

The 2013 CONCACAF Gold Cup final was played at Soldier Field in Chicago on 28 July 2013. The United States beat Panama 1-0. Referee: Joel Aguilar (El Salvador). Attendance: 57,920.

## The road to the final

The final was a rematch of the 2005 decider and the first since then not to feature Mexico. Panama had beaten Mexico 2-1 in the semi-final; the USA advanced past Honduras.

## How the match unfolded

The match was goalless until the 69th minute, when substitute Brek Shea touched in from inches out after Alejandro Bedoya's cross had beaten Panama goalkeeper Jaime Penedo. Shea had been on the pitch for just 42 seconds. The 1-0 held to the whistle.

## What the final meant

- The United States' fifth Gold Cup title.
- A famous super-sub moment: Shea's winner came 42 seconds after entering the game.
- qualification for the 2015 one-off CONCACAF Cup play-off against the 2015 Gold Cup winners.

## Match details

The final at Soldier Field in Chicago on 28 July 2013 was refereed by El Salvador's Joel Aguilar before 57,920 spectators. The USA were without suspended manager Jürgen Klinsmann on the touchline. The match stayed goalless until the 69th minute, when Brek Shea — on the field only 42 seconds after replacing Joe Corona — touched in Bedoya's cross at the far post. It was the only goal, and gave the USA a fifth Gold Cup and a place in the 2015 CONCACAF Cup play-off.
