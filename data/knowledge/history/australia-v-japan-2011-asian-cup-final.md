## The short answer

The 2011 AFC Asian Cup final was played at Khalifa International Stadium in Al Rayyan, Qatar, on 29 January 2011. Japan beat Australia 1-0 after extra time through a goal by Tadanari Lee. Referee: Ravshan Irmatov (Uzbekistan). Attendance: 37,174. Eiji Kawashima was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Australia, having joined the AFC in 2006, reached their first Asian Cup final with a powerful, physically imposing side. Japan also navigated the tournament to reach another final, drawing on their proud history in the competition.

## How the match unfolded

The final was a tense, goalless contest through ninety minutes and into extra time. In the 109th minute, substitute Tadanari Lee struck a magnificent volley into the corner to give Japan a 1-0 lead, which they held to the final whistle. It was a moment of pure quality in a tight match.

## What the final meant

- Japan's fourth Asian Cup title, cementing their status as the competition's most successful nation.
- A famous extra-time winner from Tadanari Lee that entered Asian Cup folklore.
- Qualification for the 2013 FIFA Confederations Cup as AFC's representative.
