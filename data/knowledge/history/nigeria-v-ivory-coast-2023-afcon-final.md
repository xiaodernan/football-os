## The short answer

The 2023 Africa Cup of Nations final was played at the Alassane Ouattara Stadium in Abidjan, Ivory Coast, on 11 February 2024. Hosts Ivory Coast beat Nigeria 2-1. Referee: Dahane Beida (Mauritania). Attendance: 57,094. Simon Adingra was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Nigeria reached their eighth final, drawing on a long history of AFCON success. Ivory Coast reached their fifth final and, as hosts, were determined to make the occasion count after a difficult group stage.

## How the match unfolded

Nigeria took the lead through William Troost-Ekong in the 38th minute, but Ivory Coast turned the game around after the break. Franck Kessie equalised in the 62nd minute and Sebastien Haller scored the winner in the 81st, completing a famous comeback for the hosts.

## What the final meant
