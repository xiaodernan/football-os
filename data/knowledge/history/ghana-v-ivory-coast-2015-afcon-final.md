## The short answer

The 2015 Africa Cup of Nations final was played at Estadio de Bata in Bata, Equatorial Guinea, on 8 February 2015. Ghana drew 0-0 with Ivory Coast after extra time, and Ivory Coast won 9-8 on penalties. Referee: Bakary Gassama (Gambia). Attendance: 32,857. Afriyie Acquah of Ghana was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Ghana won Group C and beat Guinea and hosts Equatorial Guinea in the quarter-final and semi-final. Ivory Coast won Group D and beat Algeria and DR Congo in the knockout rounds. The final was a rematch of the 1992 final, which Ivory Coast had also won on penalties against Ghana.

## How the match unfolded

The match was goalless through ninety minutes and extra time, with few clear chances. The only shot on target in normal time came in the 12th minute, when Yaya Touré's free kick went straight to Ghana goalkeeper Brimah Razak. Christian Atsu hit the post from 30 yards in the 25th minute, and André Ayew did likewise in the 41st. In the shoot-out, Ghana led 2-0 after Wilfried Bony and Junior Tallo missed for Ivory Coast, but Ivorian goalkeeper Boubacar Barry saved Afriyie Acquah and Frank Acheampong missed Ghana's fourth, levelling the tie. After six more penalties each, Barry saved Ghana goalkeeper Razak's attempt and then scored past him himself to seal a 9-8 win.

## What the final meant

- Ivory Coast's second Africa Cup of Nations title, twenty-three years after their first in 1992 — also won on penalties against Ghana.
- A rise from third to second in the FIFA World Rankings.
- Barry, the Ivorian goalkeeper, became the hero by saving a penalty and then scoring one himself.
- Ghana's Acquah was named man of the match despite the defeat; BBC Sport called the loss "perhaps a little harsh".
