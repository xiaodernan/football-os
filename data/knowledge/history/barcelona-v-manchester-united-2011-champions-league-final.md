## The short answer

The 2010–11 UEFA Champions League final was played at Wembley Stadium, London, on 28 May 2011 at 19:45 BST — cloudy, 15 °C, 76% humidity, before 87,695 witnesses. Barcelona 3–1 Manchester United. Goals: Pedro 27', Rooney 34', Messi 54', Villa 69'. Referee Viktor Kassai (Hungary); assistants Gábor Erős and György Ring; fourth official István Vad. UEFA Man of the Match and Fans' Man of the Match: Lionel Messi.

It was a repeat of the 2009 final — Barcelona won again, their fourth European Cup after 1992, 2006 and 2009. It was the first European Cup final at the new Wembley Stadium and the sixth at the Wembley site (1963, 1968, 1971, 1978, 1992, 2011), and one of the most dominant displays of its era.

## What the route told us

Barcelona: Group D winners on 14 points (Panathinaikos 2, Rubin Kazan 6, Copenhagen 10). Knockouts: first leg a 1–2 away loss at the Emirates against Arsenal, then 3–1 at the Camp Nou — 4–3 on aggregate; Shakhtar Donetsk 6–1 on aggregate; Real Madrid 3–1 on aggregate (2–0 away at the Bernabéu first leg, 1–1 at the Camp Nou) in the 212th El Clásico — to set up the final.

Manchester United: Group C winners on 14 points (Valencia 11, Rangers 6, Bursaspor 1). Knockouts: Marseille 2–1 on aggregate (0–0 away, 2–1 home); Chelsea 3–1 on aggregate (1–0 away at Stamford Bridge, 2–1 home); Schalke 04 6–1 on aggregate (2–0 away first leg, 4–1 home).

## How the match decided itself

United had the brighter opening exchanges — Javier Hernández went through on goal and was blocked; Villa's 19th-minute shot went just wide and his 21st-minute shot was saved by Van der Sar. Barcelona took the lead on 27' — Xavi found Pedro in the 27th minute, Pedro finished low into the corner against the run of play. Seven minutes later United equalised against the run of play — Wayne Rooney beat the press, played a one-two with Ryan Giggs, and curled fifteen yards into the far corner. Half-time: 1–1.

Second half: Barcelona increased the tempo. On 52', Messi collected a Van der Sar rebound from a Dani Alves shot — Patrice Evra cleared it off the line. On 54', Messi took the ball 20 yards out and fired into the corner. On 66' a Xavi long-range effort was saved by Van der Sar; on 69', David Villa curled into the top corner from 20 yards to seal it. United tried to pressure late: Nani's chance went wide in the 85th, Rooney's last shot went over the bar, but the game was well and truly decided.

## What the final meant

Barcelona became the first club to win three European Cups within six seasons (2006, 2009, 2011). Their fourth European Cup overall since 1992. Eric Abidal — two months after liver surgery — was given the honour of wearing the captain's armband for the trophy presentation; he lifted the cup first. Edwin van der Sar's last appearance in football. Ferguson called it the best team he had ever faced.

Wembley entered the European Cup's record book: the new stadium's first European Cup final; the site's sixth (1963, 1968, 1971, 1978, 1992, 2011).

## The wider meaning

- It was the second final between these sides — one in Rome in 2009 and this one at Wembley — both won by Barcelona. Barcelona had won the European Cup in 1992, 2006 and 2009; Manchester United had won it in 1968, 1999 and 2008.
- Manchester United were three-time champions themselves (1968, 1999, 2008) before this final.
