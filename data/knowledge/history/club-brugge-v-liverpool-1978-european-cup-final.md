## The short answer

The 1977–78 European Cup final was played at Wembley Stadium, London, on 10 May 1978 at 19:15 BST — before 92,500 witnesses. Club Brugge 0–1 Liverpool. Goal: Kenny Dalglish 64' — in through on goal from a Graeme Souness pass, past Birger Jensen. Referee Charles Corver (Netherlands).

It made Liverpool the first British team to retain the European Cup — and the European trophy a fixture in England for three of the following six years (Liverpool 1981, 1984 followed).

## What the route told us

Liverpool: defending champions — bye in first round. Second round: Dynamo Dresden 6–3 agg (5–1 H, 1–2 A); Quarter-finals: Benfica 6–2 agg (2–1 A at the Estádio da Luz, 4–1 H); Semi-finals: Borussia Mönchengladbach 4–2 agg (1–2 A at Bökelberg — Bonhof's free-kick won the first leg 2–1 for Gladbach; 3–0 at Anfield — Kennedy 6', Dalglish 35', Case 56').

Club Brugge: First round: Kuopion Palloseura 9–2 agg (4–0 away in Finland, 5–2 home); Second round: Panathinaikos 2–1 agg (2–0 H, 0–1 A); Quarter-finals: Atlético Madrid 4–3 agg (2–0 H, 2–3 A at the Vicente Calderón); Semi-finals: Juventus 2–1 agg (0–1 A at Stadio Olimpico di Torino — Bettega; 2–0 H at Jan Breydel — Vandereycken a.e.t. in the 116th minute).

## How the match decided itself

The first half was nervy, dull — Brugge chose a deep, narrow band-shape, working the counter, offside trap very explicit. Clear chances: Kennedy, Case, then Brugge's Jensen produced two crucial saves — Fairclough's run blocked, Alan Hansen's header tipped over. Half-time 0–0.

After the break, Liverpool's transition finally cracked it: substituting Steve Heighway for Case introduced width. In the 64th minute, Souness played a forward diagonal ball into the Brugge penalty area — Dalglish accepted it, controlled, and placed a low shot over Birger Jensen.

## What the final meant

Paisley's Liverpool went back-to-back — the European Cup was now firmly an English possession. Emlyn Hughes was the captain and emblematic leader of the 1970s side.
