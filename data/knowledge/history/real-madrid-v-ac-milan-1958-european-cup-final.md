## The short answer

The 1957–58 European Cup final was played at the Heysel Stadium, Brussels, on 28 May 1958. Real Madrid 3–2 AC Milan after extra time. Goals: Milan — Juan Alberto Schiaffino 59', Ernesto Grillo 77'; Real Madrid — Alfredo Di Stéfano 74', Héctor Rial 79', Paco Gento 107'. Referee Albert Alsteen (Belgium); attendance 67,000.

It was Real Madrid's third consecutive European Cup — the first European Cup hat-trick.

## What the route told us

AC Milan: Preliminary: Rapid Wien 6–6 (4–1 home, 2–5 away, replay 4–2); First round: Rangers 6–1 (4–1 away, 2–0 home); Quarter-finals: Borussia Dortmund 5–2; Semi-finals: Manchester United 5–2 (1–2 away, 4–0 home).

## How the match decided itself

Milan led twice. Schiaffino opened the scoring in the 59th minute — Madrid answered 15 minutes later through Di Stéfano. Grillo again put Milan ahead in the 77th. Di Stéfano — again. Rial for number 79'. Both pairs tied at 2. At 90 minutes, 2–2 — extra time needed. Gento settled in the 107th minute of play, in extra time. Madrid had once again prevailed.

## What the final meant

- Real Madrid 3 consecutive European Cup titles (1956, 1957, 1958).
- Climbing to a record; the first hat-trick of European Cups. And they would go on… 1959 win.
- AC Milan's first European Cup final (they would win one in 1963).
