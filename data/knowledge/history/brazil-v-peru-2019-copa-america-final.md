## The short answer

The 2019 Copa America final was played at the Maracana Stadium in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, on 7 July 2019. Hosts Brazil beat Peru 3-1 to clinch their ninth Copa America title and their first since 2007. Referee: Roberto Tobar (Chile). Attendance: 69,968. Everton was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Brazil, hosting the tournament, came through the group stage and knockout rounds with a side eager to end a twelve-year title drought. Peru also progressed through the competition to reach a first final in decades.

## How the match unfolded

Brazil controlled the final and won 3-1. Everton, the man of the match, was among the scorers as Brazil's attacking quality proved too much for Peru. A late Peru goal was a mere consolation in a game Brazil largely dictated.

## What the final meant

- Brazil's ninth Copa America title, ending a twelve-year wait since 2007.
- A home triumph at the Maracana, in front of nearly 70,000 supporters.
- Everton's emergence as a hero of the tournament, capped by his man-of-the-match award.
