## The short answer

The 2021 Africa Cup of Nations final was played at Olembe Stadium in Yaounde, Cameroon, on 6 February 2022. Senegal and Egypt drew 0-0 after extra time, and Senegal won 4-2 on penalties. Referee: Victor Gomes (South Africa). Attendance: 48,000. Mohamed Abou Gabal of Egypt was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Senegal reached their third final, having lost in 2002 and 2019, and were determined to finally lift the trophy. Egypt also navigated the tournament to reach another final, hoping to add to their honours.

## How the match unfolded

The final was a tense, goalless contest through ninety minutes and extra time. The best chance fell to Sadio Mane early, but Egypt's goalkeeper Abou Gabal saved his penalty. With no goals, the title went to penalties. Edouard Mendy made a decisive save, and Mane — having missed earlier — scored the winning kick to give Senegal the cup.

## What the final meant

- Senegal's first Africa Cup of Nations title, after two previous final defeats.
- A redemptive moment for Sadio Mane, who scored the decisive penalty after an earlier miss.
- Despite the loss, Egypt's Abou Gabal was named man of the match for his heroic goalkeeping.

## Match details

The final at Olembe Stadium in Yaounde on 6 February 2022 was refereed by Victor Gomes of South Africa before 48,000 spectators. The match was goalless through normal and extra time, with the clearest chance an early penalty for Senegal that Sadio Mane saw saved by Egypt's Abou Gabal. The decider went to penalties, where Edouard Mendy's crucial save from Mohanad Lasheen left Senegal 3-2 ahead. Mane, who had missed earlier in the shoot-out, stepped up again and scored past Gabal to seal a 4-2 victory and Senegal's first-ever AFCON title. Abou Gabal, despite being on the losing side, was named man of the match for his outstanding performance. The victory sparked massive celebrations in Dakar, with over half a million people taking part in a victory parade.
