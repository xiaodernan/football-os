## The short answer

The 2023 AFC Asian Cup final was played at Lusail Stadium in Lusail, Qatar, on 10 February 2024. Hosts and defending champions Qatar beat first-time finalists Jordan 3-1. Referee: Ma Ning (China). Attendance: 86,492. Akram Afif was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Jordan reached a first major tournament final in their history, a remarkable achievement for the side. Qatar, hosting and defending the title, also navigated the tournament to reach another final, hoping to retain their crown.

## How the match unfolded

Jordan took the lead through Yazan Al-Naimat in the 67th minute, but Qatar were awarded three penalties in the closing stages. Akram Afif converted all three, completing a hat-trick of spot-kicks and sealing a 3-1 comeback win for the hosts.

## What the final meant

- Qatar's second consecutive Asian Cup title, the first team to retain the trophy since Japan in 2004.
- A unique hat-trick of penalties from Akram Afif in a final, an extraordinary way to decide a tournament.
- A historic first final for Jordan, who despite losing announced themselves on the continental stage.
