## The short answer

The 2007 AFC Asian Cup final was played at Gelora Bung Karno Stadium in Jakarta, Indonesia, on 29 July 2007. Iraq beat Saudi Arabia 1-0. Referee: Mark Shield (Australia). Attendance: 60,000. Nashat Akram was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Iraq reached their first Asian Cup final against the odds, forced to train outside their war-torn country and troubled by transport problems and a lack of facilities. They eliminated South Korea in the semi-final. Saudi Arabia were appearing in their sixth final in seven tournaments, having won three. This was only the second all-Arab final in the competition's history.

## How the match unfolded

The only goal came in the 73rd minute, when Younis Mahmoud headed in from Hawar Mulla Mohammed's corner. Iraq held on for a 1-0 win, completing one of international football's greatest underdog stories.

## What the final meant

- Iraq's first Asian Cup title, in their first final.
- Qualification for the 2009 FIFA Confederations Cup in South Africa.
- The win united a nation at war: thousands of Iraqis took to the streets to celebrate.
- A grieving mother had said she would not bury her son, killed in a bomb attack on celebrating fans, unless Iraq won the title. They played on and did.
