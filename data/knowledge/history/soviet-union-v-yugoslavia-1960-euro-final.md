## The short answer

The first UEFA European Championship final was played at the Parc des Princes in Paris, France, on 10 July 1960. The Soviet Union beat Yugoslavia 2-1 after extra time. Referee: Arthur Ellis (England). Attendance: 17,966.

## The road to the final

The Soviet Union beat Hungary over two legs, received a walkover when Spain withdrew, then beat Czechoslovakia 3-0 in the semi-final. Yugoslavia beat Bulgaria, Portugal and hosts France.

## How the match unfolded

In rainy conditions, Yugoslavia took the lead, but the Soviet Union fought back to level and then won it 2-1 in extra time, becoming the first European champions.

## What the final meant

- The inaugural European Championship title for the Soviet Union.
- The first of three final appearances for Yugoslavia, all loses (1960, 1968).
- A tournament born in the shadow of the World Cup, now the continents premier national-team prize.
