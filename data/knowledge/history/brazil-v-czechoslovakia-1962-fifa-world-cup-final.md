## The short answer

The 1962 FIFA World Cup final was played at the Estadio Nacional in Santiago, Chile, on 17 June 1962. Defending champions Brazil beat Czechoslovakia 3-1 to retain the World Cup. Referee: Nikolay Latyshev (Soviet Union). Attendance: 68,679.

## The road to the final

Both finalists came through Group 3, where they drew 0-0. Brazil beat Mexico and Spain, then England and hosts Chile. Czechoslovakia beat Spain, then Hungary and Yugoslavia.

## How the match unfolded

Brazil won 3-1 to become only the second nation to retain the World Cup, despite playing without the injured Pele. Vava scored early, Czechoslovakia equalised, but Brazil reclaimed the lead and sealed the win with a third goal. It was the second final featuring teams that had already met in the group stage.

## What the final meant

- Brazil's second consecutive World Cup title, confirming their status as the era's dominant force.
- A retention achieved without their star Pele, who was injured in the group stage.
- Czechoslovakia's second World Cup final appearance, after 1934.
