## The short answer

The 2019 CONCACAF Gold Cup final was played at Soldier Field in Chicago, Illinois, on 7 July 2019. Mexico beat the United States 1-0. Referee: Mario Escobar (Guatemala). Attendance: 62,493. Jonathan dos Santos was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

This was the sixth Gold Cup final between Mexico and the USA, and the first since 2011. Mexico had won seven modern Gold Cups, the USA six. Both teams had navigated difficult tournaments to reach another final in their storied rivalry.

## How the match unfolded

The only goal was scored by Jonathan dos Santos in the second half. Mexico's midfielder controlled the tempo and was rewarded with the winner, and the 1-0 scoreline held to the final whistle.
