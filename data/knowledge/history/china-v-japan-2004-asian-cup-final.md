## The short answer

The 2004 AFC Asian Cup final was played at Workers' Stadium in Beijing, China, on 7 August 2004. Japan beat hosts China 3-1. Referee: Saad Kamil Al-Fadhli (Kuwait). Attendance: 62,000. Shunsuke Nakamura was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

China, as hosts, reached their first Asian Cup final since 1994 and were seeking their first title. Japan were aiming for a third crown, having won in 1992 and 2000.

## How the match unfolded

Takashi Fukunishi put Japan ahead in the 22nd minute. China equalised through Li Ming in the 31st, but Koji Nakata restored the lead in the 65th and Keiji Tamada sealed the win in stoppage time for 3-1.

## What the final meant

- Japan's third Asian Cup title, their second in four tournaments.
- A first Asian Cup final for hosts China, who had to wait longer for their first title.
- Shunsuke Nakamura, who pulled the strings in midfield, was named man of the match.
