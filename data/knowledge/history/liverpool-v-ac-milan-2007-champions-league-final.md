## The short answer

The 2006–07 UEFA Champions League final was played at the Olympic Stadium, Athens, Greece, on 23 May 2007 — partly cloudy, 24 °C, 46% humidity, before 63,000 witnesses. AC Milan beat Liverpool 2–1 — Filippo Inzaghi 45' (from Andrea Pirlo's free kick, deflecting off Inzaghi), Filippo Inzaghi 82', Dirk Kuyt 89'. Referee was Herbert Fandel (Germany). UEFA Man of the Match was Filippo Inzaghi. This was the rematch of the 2005 final at Istanbul.

## What the route told us

Liverpool came through Group C as winners with 13 points; knockouts: Barcelona 2–2 (1–2 away at Camp Nou, 1–0 home — away-goals rule); PSV Eindhoven 4–0; Chelsea 1–1, won on penalties 4–1. Milan came through Group H as winners with 10 points after Calciopoli-driven qualifying complication (they had finished second in Serie A but were docked 30 points; they qualified in Q3 with wins over Red Star Belgrade 1–0 and 2–1). Knockouts: Celtic 1–0 (Kaká's extra time strike at 93'); Bayern Munich 4–2; Manchester United 5–3 (away win 3–2, away second leg win 3–0). It was Milan's seventh European Cup title, Liverpool's seventh European Cup final appearance.

## How the match decided itself

Andrea Pirlo's free kick found Inzaghi's body; it took a deflection off him into the net — Milan's opener. Milan's second, 82nd minute: Inzaghi's second of the match. Liverpool pulled one back in the 89th minute through Dirk Kuyt, but the clock was out. Milan held the scoreline.

## What the final meant

It was Milan's seventh European Cup; Carlo Ancelotti's side had earned their way into it through third qualifying after the Calciopoli-appointed 30-point docking (which, after appeals, reduced to a Serie A finish that won the title and a Champions League entry via Q3 rather than direct entry). It was Milan's seventh title; eleven finals; third in five years.
