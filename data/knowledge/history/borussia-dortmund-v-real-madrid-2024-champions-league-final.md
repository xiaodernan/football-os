## The short answer

The 2023–24 UEFA Champions League final was played at Wembley Stadium, London, on 1 June 2024 at 20:00 — cloudy, 18 °C, 54% humidity, before 86,212 witnesses. Borussia Dortmund 0–2 Real Madrid. Goals: Dani Carvajal 74' (Kroos corner, headed in), Vinícius Júnior 83' (Carvajal assist). Referee Slavko Vinčić (Slovenia); assistants Tomaž Klančnik and Andraž Kovačič; fourth official François Letexier; VAR Nejc Kajtazović. UEFA Man of the Match: Dani Carvajal.

Real Madrid won their record-extending 15th European Cup — their sixth in eleven seasons — the first club to lift the European Cup without losing a match, and also lifted La Liga this season: the double. Toni Kroos played his last club match in this final.

## What the route told us

Dortmund: Group F winners (11 points — PSG 0–2 away opening loss, Milan 0–0 H, Newcastle 1–0 A and 2–0 H, Milan 3–1 A, PSG 1–1 H). Knockouts: PSV Eindhoven 3–1 on aggregate (1–1 away, 2–0 home); Atlético Madrid 5–4 on aggregate (1–2 away first leg at the Metropolitano in Madrid, 4–2 home — turned from 1–4 aggregate down in second half to 5–4 winner); Paris Saint-Germain 2–0 on aggregate (1–0 home at the Westfalenstadion via Füllkrug, 1–0 away at the Parc des Princes via Hummels).

Real Madrid: Group C winners on 18 points — won all six games (Union Berlin 1–0 H, Napoli 3–2 A, Braga 2–1 A, 3–0 H, Napoli 4–2 H, Union Berlin 3–2 A). Knockouts: RB Leipzig 2–1 on aggregate (1–0 away first leg, 1–1 home); Manchester City 4–4 on aggregate, advanced on penalties (3–3 home, 1–1 away — first Champions League shoot-out since 2016; Andriy Lunin saved from Bernardo Silva and Kovačić, only Luka Modrić missing, Rüdiger winning penalty); Bayern Munich 4–3 on aggregate (2–2 away at the Allianz Arena, 2–1 home at the Bernabéu — Joselu scored in the 88th and 90+1' to turn the game from an aggregate defeat into a win).

## How the match decided itself

Adeyemi had two chances to open the scoring for Dortmund — a one-on-one against Courtois in the 21st and a difficult angle five minutes later — both denied. Outside the box from the top by Adeyemi on 58' missed high, a Füllkrug header on 66' saved by Courtois; Carvajal finally broke the deadlock in the 74th — Kroos's corner from the left, Carvajal's header at the near post. Vinícius finished a cut-back from Carvajal in the 83rd — 2–0. Dortmund thought they had pulled one back through Füllkrug (88th minute) but were offside by a heel; the match ended amid 53 arrests across Wembley and a pitch-invasion fine of £1,000 issued to a Ukrainian fan under the Football (Offences) Act.

## What the final meant

It was Real Madrid's 15th European Cup/Champions League — their sixth in eleven seasons. It was Carlo Ancelotti's fifth Champions League title as a manager — the most by any coach — and Toni Kroos's final club match; Kroos joined Modrić, Nacho and Carvajal on six Champions League titles, tying Paco Gento's all-time record. Madrid also completed the double with La Liga.

Dortmund: their third final (1997 won, 2013 lost 1–2 to Bayern, 2024 lost 0–2 to Madrid). Terzić stepped down within two weeks of the final; Şahin succeeded him.
