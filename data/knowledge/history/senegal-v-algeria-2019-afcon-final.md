## The short answer

The 2019 Africa Cup of Nations final was played at Cairo International Stadium in Cairo, Egypt, on 19 July 2019. Algeria beat Senegal 1-0. Referee: Sidi Alioum (Cameroon). Attendance: 75,000. Rais M'Bolhi was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Senegal reached another final, hoping to build on their previous appearances. Algeria also navigated the tournament to reach the decider, aiming to add a second continental crown to the one they won in 1990.

## How the match unfolded

Algeria won 1-0 through a goal from Baghdad Bounedjah, a fortunate strike that proved decisive. Senegal could not find an equaliser, and Algeria held on to secure their second Africa Cup of Nations title and their first in twenty-nine years.

## What the final meant

- Algeria's second Africa Cup of Nations title, their first since 1990.
- The end of a twenty-nine-year wait for continental glory.
- A heroic goalkeeping display from Rais M'Bolhi, named man of the match.
