## The short answer

The 2019 Africa Cup of Nations final was played at Cairo International Stadium in Cairo, Egypt, on 19 July 2019. Algeria beat Senegal 1-0. Referee: Sidi Alioum (Cameroon). Attendance: 75,000. Raïs M'Bolhi was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Algeria, managed by Djamel Belmadi, went through the tournament unbeaten, with a memorable 5-0 win over Nigeria in the quarter-finals. Senegal, coached by Aliou Cissé, topped Group A and beat Uganda, Benin and Tunisia in the knockout rounds, reaching a first African final since 2002.

## How the match unfolded

Baghdad Bounedjah scored after just two minutes, capitalising on a mistake to fire Algeria ahead — a goal the Guardian described as a "lucky strike". Senegal pressed for an equaliser but could not breach Raïs M'Bolhi in the Algeria goal, and the 1-0 scoreline held to the final whistle.

## What the final meant

- Algeria's second Africa Cup of Nations title, and their first since 1990 — ending a 29-year wait.
- A first AFCON final for Senegal since 2002, when they lost to Cameroon.
- M'Bolhi's saves earned him the man of the match award and a place in African football folklore.
- Algeria went on to win the 2021 UEFA-interrupted CAF cycle's next edition was not their own, but the 2019 crown confirmed their return to the continent's elite.
