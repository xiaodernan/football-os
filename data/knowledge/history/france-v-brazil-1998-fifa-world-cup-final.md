## The short answer

The 1998 FIFA World Cup final was played at the Stade de France in Saint-Denis, just outside Paris, on 12 July 1998. Hosts France beat defending champions Brazil 3-0. Goals: Zinedine Zidane 27' and 45+1' (France), Emmanuel Petit 90+3' (France). Referee: Said Belqola (Morocco). Attendance: 80,000. An estimated 1.3 billion watched on television.

## The road to the final

Brazil topped Group A (two wins, one defeat), then beat Chile in the round of 16, Denmark in the quarter-final and the Netherlands on penalties in the semi-final. France won all three Group C matches, then beat Paraguay, Italy and Croatia in the knockout rounds.

## How the match unfolded

Before the match, attention focused on the fitness of Brazil's Ronaldo, who was initially left out of the starting line-up before being restored just before kick-off. France took the lead shortly before half-time when Zidane headed in an in-swinging corner. He headed a second from another corner just before the interval. Petit sealed the 3-0 win in stoppage time after a pass from Patrick Vieira.

## What the final meant

- France's first World Cup title, becoming the seventh nation to win the tournament.
- Zidane announced himself as the game's defining player with two headed goals in a final.
- Ronaldo was awarded the Golden Ball as the tournament's outstanding player despite the loss.
- France went on to win UEFA Euro 2000, completing a rare double.
