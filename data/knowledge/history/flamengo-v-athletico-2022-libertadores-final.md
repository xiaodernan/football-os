## The short answer

The 2022 Copa Libertadores final was played at Estadio Monumental Isidro Romero Carbo in Guayaquil, Ecuador, on 29 October 2022. Flamengo beat fellow Brazilians Athletico Paranaense 1-0. Referee: Patricio Loustau (Argentina). Attendance: 42,517. Gabriel Barbosa was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

This was the 63rd edition of CONMEBOL's top-tier South American club tournament. Flamengo came through Group H and knocked out Deportes Tolima, Corinthians and Vélez Sarsfield on the way to the decider. Athletico Paranaense won Group B before eliminating Libertad, Estudiantes and Palmeiras. It was an all-Brazilian final, the first such single-match final staged in Ecuador.

## How the match unfolded

The only goal came in first-half added time, at 45+4 minutes. Gabriel Barbosa side-footed in a low cross from the right at the far post to give Flamengo a 1-0 lead. Athletico had Pedro Henrique sent off in the 43rd minute and, despite a strong second-half push, could not find an equaliser. Flamengo held on for the clean sheet.

## What the final meant

- Flamengo's third Copa Libertadores title, adding to 1981 and 2019.
- Qualification for the 2022 FIFA Club World Cup and the 2023 Recopa Sudamericana.
- A place in the 2023 Copa Libertadores group stage.
- Under CONMEBOL's rules from 2019 onward, this was a single match at a pre-chosen venue: the last two-legged final had been the famous 2018 Superfinal.

## Match details

The final at Estadio Monumental in Guayaquil on 29 October 2022 was refereed by Patricio Loustau of Argentina before 42,517 spectators. Both finalists were Brazilian: Flamengo, managed by Dorival Júnior, and Athletico Paranaense, managed by Luiz Felipe Scolari. Athletico were reduced to ten men when Pedro Henrique was dismissed in the 43rd minute, and in first-half added time Gabriel Barbosa converted a right-sided low cross at the far post. That proved to be the only goal. Barbosa, who had been involved in almost every attacking move, was named man of the match. The victory gave Flamengo a third Libertadores crown and a place at the 2022 FIFA Club World Cup, while Athletico had to settle for a runners-up finish after a tournament in which they had beaten Palmeiras in the semi-finals.
