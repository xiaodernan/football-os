## The short answer

The 2019–20 UEFA Champions League final was played at the Estádio da Luz, Lisbon, on 23 August 2020 — clear night, 25 °C, 53% humidity, behind closed doors because of the COVID-19 pandemic. Bayern Munich beat Paris Saint-Germain 1–0 — Kingsley Coman 59', assisted by Joshua Kimmich. Referee was Daniele Orsato (Italy). UEFA Man of the Match was Kingsley Coman.

This was the second continental treble for Bayern, their second in seven years (2013, 2020). They entered the final on a 20-match winning streak and unbeaten in 2020, having not lost in 29 consecutive matches; they were attempting to claim any European competition with a 100% winning record, the first team ever to do so — six wins from six in the group, then five from five in the single-leg knockouts through to the final.

## What the route told us

Paris Saint-Germain came through Group A as winners with 16 points; knockouts: Borussia Dortmund 3–2 agg (1–2 away, 2–0 home); Atalanta 2–1 agg (1–2 in Bergamo, 1–0 at the Estádio da Luz in the final-eight); RB Leipzig 3–0 agg. Bayern Munich came through Group B as winners with 18 points; knockouts: Chelsea 7–1 agg (3–0 away, 4–1 at home); Barcelona 8–2 agg (4–0 in Barcelona, 4–2 on August 14 in Lisbon — the firestorm); Lyon 3–0 in the semi-finals.

The final was played on a Sunday because of COVID scheduling. It was the first European Cup final in the competition era — since its rebranding to Champions League in 1993 — to be played on a Sunday, and the first since 2009 not to be played on a Saturday. It was also the first final of the competition to be played after June. Originally scheduled for Istanbul on 30 May 2020, it was postponed by the COVID pandemic and relocated to Lisbon in a final-eight tournament on 17 June.

## How the match decided itself

PSG arrived with Neymar and Edinson Cavani ruled out; the published record lists both as absent. Bayern edged a narrow game more tactically than freely. Coman's 59th-minute goal — a header from Joshua Kimmich's cross — settled it; PSG could not find the equaliser. Robert Lewandowski, who had produced 15 goals in the competition proper, did not score on the night.

## What the final meant

It was Bayern's second treble; they also won the Bundesliga and DFB-Pokal earlier in the same cycle. It was Bayern's sixth European Cup — their eleventh final in European competition, tying Milan for the second-most appearances in a final behind Real Madrid (who were on sixteen). Hansi Flick — assistant manager before his promotion to head coach, and a former Bayern player who lost the 1987 European Cup final against Porto — became the 15th individual to appear in a Champions League final as both player and manager. Kingsley Coman, a former PSG academy product, scored against his former club inside the empty stands in Lisbon.

PSG, fresh off their own full house of domestic trophies (Ligue 1 decided on a PPG ratio after the season was curtailed; Coupe de France; Coupe de la Ligue; Trophée des Champions), went home empty. They had played 110 prior Champions League matches coming into the final — more than any debutant in the competition's history.
