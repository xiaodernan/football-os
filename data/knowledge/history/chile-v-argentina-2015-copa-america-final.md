## The short answer

The 2015 Copa America final was played at the Estadio Nacional in Santiago, Chile, on 4 July 2015. Hosts Chile drew 0-0 with Argentina after extra time and won 4-1 on penalties. Referee: Wilmar Roldan (Colombia). Attendance: 45,693. Arturo Vidal was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Chile, playing at home, came through a group and knockout path to reach their first final in decades. Argentina, with a star-studded attack, also progressed through the tournament to set up a classic decider between the two sides.

## How the match unfolded

The final was a tense, goalless contest through ninety minutes and extra time. Neither side could break the deadlock, so the title was decided by a penalty shoot-out. Chile converted four of their kicks while Argentina scored only one, giving the hosts their first Copa America title.

## What the final meant

- Chile's first Copa America title, won on home soil after decades of near misses.
- Qualification for the 2017 FIFA Confederations Cup in Russia.
- A famous vindication for a generation of Chilean players who had long been runners-up.
