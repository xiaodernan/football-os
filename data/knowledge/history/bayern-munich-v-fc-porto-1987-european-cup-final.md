## The short answer

The 1986–87 European Cup final was played at the Praterstadion, Vienna, on 27 May 1987 at 20:15 CEST — before 57,500 witnesses. Bayern München 1–2 FC Porto. Goals: Porto — Rabah Madjer 77' (a famous backheel), Juary 80'; Bayern — Ludwig Kögl 25'. Referee Alexis Ponnet (Belgium).

## What the route told us

Bayern: First round: PSV Eindhoven 2–0 (2–0 away, 0–0 home); Second round: Austria Wien 3–1 (2–0 away, 1–1 home); Quarter-finals: Anderlecht 7–2 (5–0 away, 2–2 home); Semi-finals: Real Madrid 4–2 (4–1 home at the Bernabéu, 0–1 away).

Porto: First round: Rabat Ajax 10–0 (9–0 home, 1–0 away); Second round: Vítkovice 3–1 (0–1 away, 3–0 home); Quarter-finals: Brøndby IF 2–1 (1–0 home, 1–1 away); Semi-finals: Dynamo Kyiv 4–2 (2–1 away, 2–1 home).

## How the match decided itself

Bayern led first from Kögl's 25th-minute header. Porto found themselves 1–0 down mid-match and needed a revival. With 2 minutes to play, Rabah Madjer produced what is considered the most famous backheel in European Cup final history — 1–1. Three minutes later, Juary volleyed in from the box. Bayern's response — Matthäus and company — was not enough.

## What the final meant

Porto won their first European Cup — still their only one (since 2004). Artur Jorge was the manager. Bayern lost the final with missing key players: Klaus Augenthaler banned; Roland Wohlfarth injured; Hans Dorfner injured. The sequence Bayern losing a 1–0 lead in an EC final recurred 1999 against Manchester United.
