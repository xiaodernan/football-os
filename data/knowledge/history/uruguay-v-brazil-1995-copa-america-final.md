## The short answer

The 1995 Copa America final was played at the Estadio Centenario in Montevideo, Uruguay, on 23 July 1995. Uruguay and reigning World champions Brazil drew 1-1, and Uruguay won 5-3 on penalties. Referee: Arturo Brizio Carter (Mexico). Attendance: 60,000.

## The road to the final

Uruguay, playing at home, reached their fourth final and continued a remarkable record of winning every major tournament staged in the country. Brazil, the reigning World champions, also reached their fourth final, setting up the third decider between the two sides.

## How the match unfolded

The final finished 1-1 after ninety minutes, forcing a penalty shoot-out. Uruguay held their nerve to win 5-3, extending their historic run of winning every international tournament held on Uruguayan soil, a streak that included the 1930 World Cup.
