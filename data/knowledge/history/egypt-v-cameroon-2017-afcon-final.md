## The short answer

The 2017 Africa Cup of Nations final was played at Stade de l'Amitie in Libreville, Gabon, on 5 February 2017. Cameroon beat Egypt 2-1. Referee: Janny Sikazwe (Zambia). Attendance: 38,250. Benjamin Moukandjo was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Cameroon finished as runners-up in Group A before beating Senegal and Ghana in the knockout rounds. Egypt won Group D and then beat Morocco and Burkina Faso to reach the final, arriving as strong favourites after a flawless campaign.

## How the match unfolded

Egypt started strongly and took the lead through Mohamed Elneny after 22 minutes. Cameroon had more possession but lacked a cutting edge in the first half. After the break, substitute Nicolas Nkoulou equalised in the 59th minute, and Vincent Aboubakar scored two minutes from the end to complete a 2-1 comeback for Cameroon.

## What the final meant

- Cameroon's fifth Africa Cup of Nations title, matching Egypt's record at the time.
- A dramatic late comeback that overturned a side that had won every previous match in the tournament.
- Qualification to represent CAF at the 2017 FIFA Confederations Cup in Russia.

## Match details

The final at Stade de l'Amitie in Libreville on 5 February 2017 was refereed by Janny Sikazwe of Zambia before 38,250 spectators. Egypt, who had won every match leading up to the final, struck first through Mohamed Elneny in the 22nd minute and led at half time. Cameroon had more possession but lacked potency in the opening period. The introduction of substitute Nicolas Nkoulou changed the game: he equalised in the 59th minute, and with two minutes remaining Vincent Aboubakar scored the winner to seal a 2-1 comeback. Benjamin Moukandjo, whose energy embodied the Cameroonian side, was named man of the match. The victory gave Cameroon a fifth AFCON crown and a place at the 2017 FIFA Confederations Cup in Russia.
