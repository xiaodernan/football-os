## The short answer

The 2019 AFC Asian Cup final was played at Zayed Sports City Stadium in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, on 1 February 2019. Qatar beat Japan 3-1. Referee: Ravshan Irmatov (Uzbekistan). Attendance: 36,776. Akram Afif was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Japan arrived as the competition's most successful nation with four titles, won all six of their matches and had never lost an Asian Cup final. Qatar were playing in a first final, and had not conceded a goal in the prior six matches of the tournament. Félix Sánchez's young side had beaten Iraq, South Korea and hosts United Arab Emirates on the way to the decider.

## How the match unfolded

Almoez Ali opened the scoring in the 12th minute with a bicycle kick, his ninth goal of the tournament, taking the Asian Cup record. Abdulaziz Hatem made it 2-0 in the 27th with a long-range shot. Takumi Minamino pulled one back in the 69th — the first goal Qatar had conceded all tournament. A VAR review awarded a late penalty for a handball by Maya Yoshida, and Akram Afif converted it in the 83rd minute to settle the match at 3-1.

## What the final meant

- Qatar's first Asian Cup title, making them the ninth country to win the competition.
- Japan's first ever Asian Cup final defeat, ending a perfect seven-match run.
- Almoez Ali's nine goals broke Ali Daei's long-standing record of eight in one Asian Cup.
- The first Asian Cup final to use the video assistant referee.

## Match details

The final at Zayed Sports City Stadium on 1 February 2019 was refereed by Ravshan Irmatov of Uzbekistan, with China's Ma Ning as fourth official, before 36,776 spectators. Japan made two early set-piece chances but could not convert. Qatar struck first through Almoez Ali's acrobatic finish, then Hatem's 25-yard shot. Japan pressed after the break and Minamino's close-range goal made it 2-1, but the VAR-awarded penalty was converted by Afif. Qatar won all seven of their matches in the tournament without conceding until the final. Afif, with one goal and two assists, was named man of the match.
