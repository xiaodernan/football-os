## The short answer

The 1958–59 European Cup final was played at the Neckarstadion, Stuttgart, on 3 June 1959 at 18:00 CEST — before 72,000 witnesses. Real Madrid 2–0 Stade de Reims. Goals: Enrique Mateos 1', Alfredo Di Stéfano 47'. Referee Albert Dusch (West Germany).

It was Real Madrid's fourth consecutive European Cup — Madrid's fifth straight final win.

## How the match decided itself

The match was controlled by Real Madrid from the first minute — Mateos scored in minute one. The rest of the game was a rout.

## What the final meant

- Real Madrid 4 consecutive European Cups (1956, 1957, 1958, 1959).
- Reims' second European Cup final — a return at 1956 calibre.
- One more season — 1960 Hampden — and Madrid completed the five-in-a-row.
