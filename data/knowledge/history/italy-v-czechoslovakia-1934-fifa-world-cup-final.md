## The short answer

The 1934 FIFA World Cup final was played at the Stadio Nazionale PNF in Rome, Italy, on 10 June 1934. Hosts Italy beat Czechoslovakia 2-1 after extra time. Referee: Ivan Eklind (Sweden). Attendance: 55,000. Temperatures approached 40 degrees Celsius.

## The road to the final

The 1934 tournament was a straight knockout. Italy beat the United States, Spain (a replay) and Austria en route; Czechoslovakia beat Romania, Switzerland and Germany.

## How the match unfolded

Czechoslovakia took the lead and looked set to win, but Italy equalised late to force extra time. In the additional period, with the heat taking its toll, Italy scored again to seal a 2-1 victory. It was a comeback win for the hosts in punishing conditions.

## What the final meant

- Italy's first World Cup title, won on home soil.
- The second World Cup final, four years after Uruguay's inaugural win.
- A triumph for Vittorio Pozzo's side that they would repeat in 1938.
