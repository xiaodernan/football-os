## The short answer

The 1999 Copa America final was played at the Estadio Defensores del Chaco in Asuncion, Paraguay, on 18 July 1999. Brazil beat Uruguay 3-0. Referee: Oscar Ruiz (Colombia). Attendance: 30,000. The win gave Brazil their sixth Copa America title.

## The road to the final

Brazil came through the group stage and the knockout rounds with a side full of attacking talent, reaching yet another final in a run of sustained success. Uruguay, with a proud Copa history, also progressed through the tournament to contest the decider.

## How the match unfolded

Brazil produced a commanding performance to win 3-0. Their attacking players found the net three times while the defence kept a clean sheet, controlling the final from start to finish and never allowing Uruguay to get back into the contest.
