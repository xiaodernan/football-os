## The short answer

The 2015 AFC Asian Cup final was played at Stadium Australia in Sydney on 31 January 2015. Australia beat South Korea 2-1 after extra time. Referee: Alireza Faghani (Iran). Attendance: 76,385. Trent Sainsbury was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

South Korea had reached three previous Asian Cup finals in the knockout era (1972, 1980, 1988), losing all three, though they had won the tournament twice before that era (1956, 1960). Australia, having moved from Oceania to the AFC in 2006, were in their second Asian Cup final, having lost in 2011.

## How the match unfolded

Massimo Luongo put Australia ahead in the 45th minute. South Korea equalised deep into stoppage time through Son Heung-min in the 90+1st minute, forcing extra time. In the 105th minute, substitute James Troisi scored the winning goal to make it 2-1.

## What the final meant

- Australia's first Asian Cup title, as hosts.
- Qualification for the 2017 FIFA Confederations Cup in Russia.
- A first Asian Cup final defeat for South Korea in the knockout era, extending their run of final losses in that format to four.
- Trent Sainsbury, a central defender, was named man of the match.

## Match details

The final at Stadium Australia in Sydney on 31 January 2015 was refereed by Iran's Alireza Faghani before 76,385 spectators. Australia, managed by Ange Postecoglou, took the lead just before half-time through Luongo. South Korea, coached by Uli Stielike, pressed late and Son Heung-min's stoppage-time equaliser forced extra time. In the second half of extra time, substitute Troisi found the winner. Defenders Sainsbury and Spiranovic were central to Australia's defensive effort, and Sainsbury was named man of the match. The win gave Australia their first Asian Cup and a place at the 2017 Confederations Cup, while South Korea's knockout-era final drought continued.
