## The short answer

The 2005 FIFA Confederations Cup final was played at Waldstadion in Frankfurt, Germany, on 29 June 2005. Brazil beat Argentina 4-1. Referee: Ľuboš Micheľ (Slovakia). Attendance: 45,591. Ronaldinho was named man of the match.

## The road to the final

Brazil and Argentina had met in the Copa América final the previous year. Both were among the tournament's favourites, and their semi-final wins set up a South American showpiece in Germany.

## How the match unfolded

Adriano scored in the 11th minute, Kaká made it 2-0 in the 16th, Ronaldinho added a third in the 47th, and Adriano grabbed his second in the 63rd. Pablo Aimar pulled one back for Argentina in the 65th, but Brazil's 4-1 lead was never threatened.

## What the final meant

- Brazil's first Confederations Cup title since 1999 and third overall.

## Match details

The final at Frankfurt's Waldstadion on 29 June 2005 was refereed by Slovakia's Ľuboš Micheľ before 45,591 spectators. Brazil, managed by Carlos Alberto Parreira, produced a dazzling attacking performance against their 2004 Copa América final opponents. Adriano opened the scoring in the 11th minute and Kaká made it 2-0 five minutes later. Ronaldinho, the captain, made it 3-0 just after half-time, and Adriano's second in the 63rd minute made it 4-0. Aimar's 65th-minute reply was no more than a consolation. Ronaldinho was named man of the match. It was Brazil's third Confederations Cup crown and their first since 1999.
