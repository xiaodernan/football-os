## The short answer

The 2008–09 UEFA Champions League final was played at the Stadio Olimpico, Rome, Italy, on 27 May 2009 at 20:45 CEST — clear night, 23 °C, 71 % humidity — in front of 62,467 witnesses. Barcelona beat Manchester United 2–0 to lift their third European Cup and close out the first continental treble by a Spanish club. Goals: Samuel Eto'o 10' (Iniesta's precisely weighted pass, composed finish from the edge of the box — Barcelona's captain that night, in a sense, because Iniesta drove them), Lionel Messi 70' (Eto'o assisted, Messi slotted to van der Sar's left). Referee Massimo Busacca (Switzerland); UEFA Man of the Match Xavi; Fans' Man of the Match Lionel Messi. The match was refereed with an unusually light touch — it was the first Champions League final in which neither team held a booked card before stoppage time.

## What the route told us

Barcelona came through the third qualifying round by 4–1 aggregate against Wisła Kraków (4–0 at the Camp Nou, 0–1 in Kraków). They won Group C on 13 points — 3–1 at Sporting CP, 2–1 at Shakhtar Donetsk, 5–0 at Basel, 1–1 at home to Basel, 5–2 at Sporting CP, 2–3 at home to Shakhtar (a meaningless defeat in a group sealed). In the knockouts: Olympique Lyon 6–3 on aggregate (1–1 at the Stade de Gerland; 5–2 at home), Bayern Munich 5–1 on aggregate (4–0 at the Camp Nou; 1–1 in Munich) and Chelsea 1–1 on away goals (0–0 at the Camp Nou, 1–1 at Stamford Bridge). Manchester United qualified directly to the group stage; they won Group E on 10 points (0–0 at home to Villarreal, 3–0 away at AaB, 3–0 at home to Celtic, 1–1 away at Celtic, 0–0 away at Villarreal, 2–2 at home to AaB). In the knockouts: Inter Milan 2–0 (0–0 away, 2–0 at home), Porto 3–2 (2–2 at home, 1–0 away), Arsenal 4–1 (1–0 at home, 3–1 away at the Emirates). United were defending champions (Moscow 2008 final on penalties against Chelsea).

## What the final meant

For Barcelona, this was their third European Cup (1992, 2006, 2009), the first for a Spanish club to complete La Liga + Copa del Rey + Champions League in a single season, Pep Guardiola's first season in charge, the end of Pep's six-season contractual arc, and the only meeting between Messi and Ronaldo at a European Cup final. For Manchester United, this was their third consecutive final — after 2007 (lost 1–2 to AC Milan) and 2008 (won Moscow on penalties) — and the quest for a second European Cup in three seasons ended here. For Spain, the continental treble started in 2009 and the decade's aesthetic benchmark was set in Rome.
