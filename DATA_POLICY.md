# DATA_POLICY — FOOTBALL OS

## 1. Provenance classes

Every persisted row carries `data_origin`:

- `synthetic` — generated by our own world builder (fictional names, no real-world claim). Displayed as **DEMO DATA**.
- `licensed` — obtained through a contracted provider behind `FootballDataProvider`/`NewsProvider`, with provider id, licence ref, retrieved-at.
- `open` — legally open/public data with source URL and citation.
- `user` — uploaded/created by a user; the user is the data controller of that content, we are the processor.

Mixed provenance in one visual surface is forbidden; each card labels its class.

## 2. Never do

Scrape paid/protected databases (Opta, StatsBomb, Sportradar, FC/FM data). Copy player photos, club crests, broadcast video, or any EA/FIFA/FM asset. Imply official affiliation. Invent statistics when a metric is unavailable — render `Data unavailable` instead.

## 3. Personal data

Categories collected: account (email, display name), football identity (position, age band, attributes, goals), training logs, optional self-reported physical data, optional user-uploaded video.

Purposes: deliver the requested training/coach function, store the user's progression history, and — only with explicit consent — community/SEO publication.

Rights implemented as endpoints, not promises: `POST /me/export` (JSON archive), `DELETE /me` (hard delete + cascade of UGC, anonymisation of aggregate XP), consent ledger, visibility toggle per entity, deletion of individual videos with storage purge.

## 4. Minors

Default-deny: private profiles, no precise location, no contact info, no public video, no AI scouting report visible outside the owning academy, parental-consent flag gates any publication. Data minimisation on signup (age band, not DOB, unless a licensed academy needs it).

## 5. Retention & multi-tenancy

UGC retained until deletion request; provider payloads retained per licence terms with `retrieved_at` and expiry; organisations (B2B) get tenant-scoped rows via `organization_id`, and offboarding produces export then purge within 30 days.

## 6. AI outputs

Model-derived assessments are labelled `AI-generated`/`Estimated` with confidence and source list, are not scouting certification, and not medical diagnosis (see `AI_POLICY.md`).
